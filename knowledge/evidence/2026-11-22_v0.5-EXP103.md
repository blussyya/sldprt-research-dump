# EXP-103: the dense fit check, finished

2026-11-22. The curve fitter now checks 8 more points per interval once its 3-point check passes,
and always reports an error that includes them. That fixes edge 826 on head_back (EXP-087) and
turned up two things the 3-point check had hidden on `dev`:

- **Edge 844 on head_back had a 1.1e-6 m hook** at one end while reporting 8.9e-9 m. SP_CURVE fits
  pinned their ends to the vertices, and this tolerant vertex sits 1.1e-6 m off the curve. Fixed:
  an end is left free when its vertex is past the 1e-8 m bar but within the edge's own tolerance.
  The fit is now 2.3e-9 m.
- **Four head_back blends now report over the bar** (up to 1.54e-8 m, from about 5.5e-9). Their
  spines are the same curves as on `dev`, to 1e-13 m. The rise comes from the last few tenths of a
  micron before a T limit, where the exact spine itself can't be found to 1e-8 m, so neither
  number is a real accuracy there. They stay reported as over.
- **A pinned end's distance from the curve is now part of the fit error.** The blind disprove check
  found a vertex 1e-4 m off its curve reported as 3.4e-9 m, on `dev` and on the branch alike. With
  it counted, two online parts show vertices far outside their tolerances, 1.57e-4 m (`0739c559`)
  and 4.49e-4 m (`f0b9a118`), which `dev` hid. **Their exact STEP export now refuses**, by the
  existing 1e-6 m limit, where `dev` wrote a file with that hook in it.

The work started on converter branch `wip/dense-fit-check` (EXP-087), was carried on by an agent
on `wip/dense-fit-check-rebased` until a usage limit stopped it mid-edit, and was finished here.
Scripts and outputs: `scripts/EXP103/` (the agent's) and `scripts/EXP103/finish/` (this run).

## What changed in the converter

| change | why | where it was shown |
|---|---|---|
| Dense pass in `fitPoints`: 8 more samples per interval once the 3 cheap ones pass | an error can sit between the cheap samples | edge 826, EXP-087; test `fit.test.js` |
| `fitPoints` always reports a dense-checked error, even out of rounds | before, running out of rounds right after the cheap check passed returned the cheap error | test: a fit 3.0e-7 m off reported 6.25e-14 m on `dev` |
| `polyFn` seeds each exact point from its own segment's start | seeded from the last call, a point could land a thread turn away (3e-3 m on `db8a97e2`) | EXP-087 |
| The corrector checks its feet and that Newton converged, and retries with the damped solver | it used to return wherever Newton stopped: blend 3369's bad points were 3e-5 m off a foot clamped at a knot edge | EXP-087; blend 3369 now 2.21e-9 m (`../after-measure.txt`) |
| A chart chord the corrector can't settle goes to the march | edge 839, a pipe half-circle with 5 chart points | agent's note, not remeasured here |
| B-spline extension past the edge uses the exact mixed derivative | a differenced one is off by 1.1e-10 relative | test: `dev` 1.13e-10, branch under 2e-11 |
| A B-spline foot that lands on the knot-range edge carries on into the extension | the clamped foot's tangent plane is wrong by more than 1e-8 m a few units out | agent's note; spring fixed by it (below) |
| A swept surface carries on straight past the end of an **open** B-spline section | the polynomial continuation is 4.5e-5 m off blend 3416's chart points, the straight one 8e-17 m | agent's measurement; test |
| SP_CURVE ends: left free when the vertex is past the bar but within the edge's tolerance; `reanchor` puts ends at the vertices' nearest points | edge 844 | below |
| A pinned end's gap to the curve counts in the fit error | a vertex 1e-4 m off reported 3.4e-9 m | below; test |
| The free-end rule uses the larger of the edge's tolerance and that vertex's own; on a fitted blend the plain SP_CURVE fit keeps its ends pinned | `f0b9a118`: four vertices within their own 1.05e-5 m tolerance but not the edge's 1e-6 m | below; test |

Two corrections to the agent's unfinished code, made here before measuring:

- The swept extension and the unclamped foot applied to closed and periodic sections too. A closed
  section has no end to carry on from, so they now apply to open sections only, and closed ones
  are evaluated as on `dev`.
- `fitPoints` could exit its loop between the cheap pass and the dense pass. Fixed as above.

## Edge 844 (head_back)

Edge 844 is a tolerant edge: tolerance 2.0e-5 m, vertices 2.0e-5 and 2.2e-5 m (`e844tol.out`). Its
curve is an SP_CURVE on a blend, fitted by `reanchor` along one section of the fitted blend
(constant u). Replaying `reanchor` step by step from the captured input reproduces the converter
to the digit: 8.901e-9 m on `dev`, 1.142e-8 m on the WIP (`e844replay.out`).

| fit | fit's own error | fit to the section, 4,001 points | ends to their vertices |
|---|---|---|---|
| both vertices pinned (`dev`) | 8.90e-9 m | **1.13e-6 m**, at the end | 0, 0 |
| both pinned, dense pass (WIP) | 1.14e-8 m | 1.13e-6 m, at the end | 0, 0 |
| pin a vertex only within 1e-8 m | 2.34e-9 m | 2.34e-9 m | 2.5e-8, 1.16e-6 m |
| same, ends at the vertices' nearest points (this branch) | 2.33e-9 m | 2.34e-9 m | 2.5e-8, 1.13e-6 m |

The fit's own error can't see the hook. It measures the curve's points against the fit, and as
the last interval is split those points bunch up next to a point the fit passes through.

The rule is gated on the edge's tolerance because the plain SP_CURVE fit, in Parasolid's own u,
puts this edge's curve 8.3e-2 m from both vertices, and pinning is what makes that fit fail and
hand over to `reanchor`. With the ends simply unpinned, that wrong curve passed at 5.8e-9 m. I
caught that on the first refit (`e844fit.out` has the final behaviour). A vertex further off than
the edge's tolerance stays pinned.

Correction (same day): I first wrote, in a code comment, that the pinned fit's error is what
reveals a wrong curve. That was only true for a gap as large as 8 cm. The blind disprove agent
moved a vertex 1e-4 m (5 times the edge tolerance) and the fit, still pinned, reported 7.1e-9 m.
On a test cylinder the same case reports 3.39e-9 m on `dev` and 3.43e-9 m on the branch before the
fix, and 1.00e-4 m after (`farvertex.out`). `fitPoints` now adds a pinned end's distance from the
curve to the error. It changes nothing where the vertex is within 1e-8 m. Where it isn't, the edge
now reports over the bar instead of passing.

Measured, not explained: no single section of our fitted blend passes near both vertices. Each
vertex's own section differs by 1.0e-5 in u, and the best compromise misses both by 5.7e-7 m
(`e844section.out`). So either our blend's sections are skewed from Parasolid's by about that
much, or one vertex is off by that much within its 2e-5 m tolerance. Which one isn't known.
(`e844end.js` was a first try at this with my own section search. It clamped at v = 0 and its
numbers are wrong; `e844replay.js` and `e844section.js` replace it.)

## Vertices outside their tolerance (`0739c559`, `f0b9a118`)

Counting a pinned end's gap (above) took these two parts from within the bar to far over it in
the sweep at 2ab8de4 (`sweep-branch-2ab8de4.txt`). Each over-bar edge, with its tolerances
(`overbar.out`, then `overbar-final.out` after the vertex-tolerance rule):

| part, edge | vertex off its curve | edge tolerance | that vertex's tolerance | now |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `0739c559` edge 6075, SP_CURVE on an offset | 1.57e-4 m | 1.0e-5 m | 4.8e-5 m | pinned, reported 1.57e-4 m |
| `f0b9a118` edge 152, SP_CURVE on a B-spline | 4.49e-4 m | 1.0e-6 m | 1.0e-6 m | pinned, reported 4.49e-4 m |
| `f0b9a118` edges 138, 139, 146, 1252 | 3.1e-6 to 3.65e-6 m | 1.0e-6 m | 1.05e-5 m | freed; 6.9e-19 to 9.8e-9 m |

The four were my rule being too strict: it looked at the edge's tolerance only, and a Parasolid
vertex's own tolerance is how far it may sit from its curves. Fixed in f939721.

Edges 6075 and 152 are real disagreements between a vertex and its curve, at about 3 and 440
times the vertex's tolerance. Which of the two is wrong isn't known. `f0b9a118` is one of the five parts with
vertices 0.1 to 1 mm off thread and degree-1 surfaces (START_HERE). `0739c559` is new. Pinned,
the exported curve hooks that far to meet the vertex. Freed, the B-rep would have a gap. Either
way the file wouldn't be the part, so the exact export refuses (its limit is 1e-6 m, EXP-085).

`dev` gives the same numbers as the WIP tip here. Each spine fitted alone with both versions
gives the same control count, and the curves are at most 3e-17 m apart (`spines.txt`); the blind
agents measured `dev` against the branch at 1e-13 m. Only the reported number changed. These
spines are fitted to 1e-9 m and run out of rounds before getting there, and the old code then
reported only its cheap samples.

What the larger number means, from both blind checks (below): over about 99.6% of each spine the
fit is within 7e-10 m of the exact spine, found with the agents' own solvers. The rise sits in the
last few tenths of a micron before the T limit, where the two supports meet at 3e-5 to 6e-5 rad.
There the exact spine can't be pinned down: a 1e-12 m change in one surface's distance moves it
2.4e-7 m at the end, and the nested spines it depends on are fitted to 1e-10 m. So 1.5e-8 m is
what the fitter measures against its own exact points, not a demonstrated error, and 5.5e-9 m
was no better founded. These blends are reported as over the bar until that zone can be checked.

## Blind checks

Two agents, each with its own evaluator and scripts and none of mine (`scripts/EXP103/prove/`,
`disprove/`).

**Claim A, edge 844 and the end rule: holds (both).**
- Prove: on `dev` the fit is 1.128e-6 m from the section at its end (6e-8 at t = 0.999, under 1e-10
  from t = 0.5 to 0.995) while reporting 8.9e-9. On the branch, 2.34e-9 m at most over 20,001
  samples; ends 2.5e-8 and 1.13e-6 m from vertices with tolerances 2.0e-5 and 2.2e-5. Control: a
  curve shifted 1e-8 and 1e-7 m reads 1.10e-8 and 1.005e-7.
- Disprove: the 1.1e-6 m belongs to the vertex pair, not to a badly chosen line: the vertices'
  own sections are 1.0e-5 apart in u, about 1.14e-6 m along the spine, and no section reaches both
  within 5.7e-7 m. Across all 48 SP_CURVE edges on head_back (all tolerant), `dev` hid bends of
  2.5e-8 to 8.7e-6 m on 11 (808 and 392 at 8.7e-6 m); the branch's worst is 9.3e-9 m, every freed
  end stays within its vertex tolerance (worst 8.7e-6 against 2e-5), and no edge got worse.
- Disprove found the hole described above (a vertex beyond the edge tolerance, pinned, unreported),
  now fixed.

**Claim B, the five spines: partly holds (both).** The curves match `dev` (1e-13 m; control points
up to 1.1e-11 m apart) and the reported numbers are as stated, but "the larger number is the true
error" isn't supported. Both agents' own solvers agree with the converter's corrector (to 3e-13 m,
disprove), so the extra points aren't bad feet. Over the well-conditioned part the fits are 2.6e-11
to 7.0e-10 m from the exact spine. In the last 0.4% the exact spine can't be determined to 1.5e-8 m
(above). Disprove puts 9821 at 3.4e-8 m 1e-7 m from its end, larger than either reported number,
with the same caveat. Blend 10349 is 9.98e-9, not about 1.5e-8 as the claim said.
