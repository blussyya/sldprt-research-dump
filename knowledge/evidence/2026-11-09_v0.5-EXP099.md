# EXP-099: repeated display face IDs and the odd surface tags

The three odd tag values were misreads. Those 56 display records have no id/tag block, and an
appearance block sits in its place. The repeated display IDs are real faces in different bodies,
not a misread. Most failed ID joins come from the converter itself: it shifts the node_ids of
LocalBodies and later partition sections, while the display keeps each section's own ids.

Measured by a Sonnet agent with its own scripts (`scripts/EXP099/`, `findings.md` has the full
numbers). I rechecked the odd-tag counts and the `d2515121` join myself. No blind prove/disprove
pass yet.

## Odd tags (measured)

| part | faces | tag read | what the bytes are |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `0739c559` | 33 | 4980815 | `4f 00 4c 00`, UTF-16 "OL" from "SOLIDWORKS Materials" |
| `7765d286` | 22 | 16368 | `f0 3f 00 00`, the top of a double 1.0 |
| `eefa906d` | 1 | 65536 | `00 00 01 00` |

- EXP-097 said 1 face each. That was wrong: it's 56 faces in 3 parts (EXP-097's own table gave 33, 22 and 1, but the text said one).
- The record boundary is right: the stored bounds check on all 410 faces of the three parts.
- The three reserved words after the normals are `0,0,1` or `0,1,0xFFFFFFFF` on 56 of 56 odd records, and `0,0,0` on 13,533 of 13,533 good ones.
- Control: re-reading the id block with the offset moved by ±1 to 16 bytes passes 0 of 13,533. At the reader's offset it passes 13,533 of 13,533.
- Converter: `src/display.js` now refuses metadata when the reserved words are nonzero or the tag is outside 4001–4010 (`dev` a256dba, with a test). Those faces keep their mesh. Tests: 67 pass, 2 skipped.

## Repeated IDs (measured)

- Corpus: 554 groups of display faces that share a rawId, in 11 parts. 2 of the groups are the misreads above.
- In 552 of 552 real groups, the members are in different bodies, so a periodic face split in two doesn't fit.
- `2f121983`: rawId 253 is a cylinder in body 2 and a plane in body 8 of one partition. The converter renumbers the plane, and the inspector maps both display faces to the cylinder.
- `a4ecc6f5`: ids 2, 31 and 228 repeat. Body 44493 comes from LocalBodies. Its display ids equal the converter's node_id minus 53508 on 15 of 15 faces.
- 3,880 of 13,532 display faces in 6 parts match a native face only after undoing `merge()`'s id shift (`src/parasolid/partition.js`): `d2515121` 2,349, `03764871` 614, `d0d61cde` 450, `ef1d5cfe` 338, `3c4752f1` 115, `a4ecc6f5` 14. Rechecked: in `d2515121` the plain join finds 180 of 2,512.
- Control: joining on id+1 hits 1.4%.
- Exports aren't affected. `convert.js` reads each display face's own tag and joins nothing by ID. The inspector and the research joins are affected, including EXP-091's 941 unjoinable faces.
