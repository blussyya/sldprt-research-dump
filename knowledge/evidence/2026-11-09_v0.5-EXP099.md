# EXP-099: repeated display face IDs and the odd surface tags

The three odd tag values were misreads. Those 56 display records have no id/tag block, and an
appearance block sits in its place. The repeated display IDs are real faces in different bodies,
not a misread. Most failed ID joins come from the converter itself: it shifts the node_ids of
LocalBodies and later partition sections, while the display keeps each section's own ids.

Measured by a Sonnet agent with its own scripts (`scripts/EXP099/`, `findings.md` has the full
numbers). I rechecked the odd-tag counts and the `d2515121` join myself.

Blind checks (2026-11-23, below): the rule holds, but the count was wrong. The three words are
nonzero on 994 records in 21 parts, not 56 in 3, and every one of them lacks the id/tag block. The
56 were only the records whose misread tag happened to be one of the three odd values. In the
three named parts every record lacks the block (342, 62 and 6), and `7765d286` has 23 of the
odd-tag records, not 22. Repeated IDs hold.

## Odd tags (measured)

| part | faces | tag read | what the bytes are |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `0739c559` | 33 | 4980815 | `4f 00 4c 00`, UTF-16 "OL" from "SOLIDWORKS Materials" |
| `7765d286` | 22 | 16368 | `f0 3f 00 00`, the top of a double 1.0 |
| `eefa906d` | 1 | 65536 | `00 00 01 00` |

- 56 faces in 3 parts, one part per value, as EXP-097's table gives.
- The record boundary is right: the stored bounds check on all 410 faces of the three parts.
- The three reserved words after the normals are `0,0,1` or `0,1,0xFFFFFFFF` on 56 of 56 odd records, and `0,0,0` on 13,533 of 13,533 good ones.
- Control: re-reading the id block with the offset moved by ±1 to 16 bytes passes 0 of 13,533. At the reader's offset it passes 13,533 of 13,533.
- Converter: `src/display.js` now refuses metadata when the reserved words are nonzero or the tag is outside 4001–4010 (`dev` a256dba, with a test). Those faces keep their mesh. Tests: 67 pass, 2 skipped.

## Repeated IDs (measured)

- Corpus: 554 groups of display faces that share a rawId, in 11 parts. 2 of the groups are the misreads above.
- In 552 of 552 real groups, the members are in different bodies, so a periodic face split in two doesn't fit.
- `2f121983`: rawId 253 is a cylinder in body 2 and a plane in body 8 of one partition. The converter renumbers the plane, and the inspector maps both display faces to the cylinder.
- `a4ecc6f5`: ids 2, 31 and 228 repeat. Body 44493 comes from LocalBodies. Its display ids equal the converter's node_id minus 53508 on 15 of 15 faces.
- 3,880 of 13,532 display faces in 6 parts match a native face only after undoing `merge()`'s id shift (`src/parasolid/partition.js`): `d2515121` 2,349, `03764871` 614, `d0d61cde` 450, `ef1d5cfe` 338, `3c4752f1` 115, `a4ecc6f5` 14. Rechecked: in `d2515121` the plain join finds 180 of 2,512.
- Control: joining on id+1 hits 1.4%.
- Exports aren't affected. `convert.js` reads each display face's own tag and joins nothing by ID. The inspector and the research joins are affected, including EXP-091's 941 unjoinable faces.

## Blind checks (2026-11-23)

Two agents, each with its own raw reader and none of this file's scripts or numbers
(`scripts/EXP099/prove/`, `scripts/EXP099/disprove/`). Both used the converter only to find face
records and inflate the stream.

**Claim 1, the odd tags: partly holds (both).**
- Holds both ways where the record reads in the usual layout: the three words after the scalar
  flag and optional scalar arrays are 0 on 13,533 of 13,533 records with a valid tag, and nonzero on
  994 records, none of which has a valid tag. Disprove searched each refused record for any u32 in
  4001 to 4010 after the words: 986 of 994 have none, and the 8 hits aren't a shifted tag block.
  Prove read the id block shifted by 1 to 32 bytes on the good records: 0 valid tags at every shift.
- Fails as worded: "nonzero on exactly these 56" is 994 records in 21 parts. In `0739c559`,
  `7765d286` and `eefa906d` every record lacks the block (342, 62, 6), and `7765d286` has 23
  odd-tag records, not 22. The other refused records read small integers or 2151088128 as the
  tag. Most of the 994 are short records with a 44-byte tail and no appearance text, so the "tag"
  is bytes of the next record; "an appearance block sits in its place" is true only for some.
- Wording: the words come after the scalar flag and the optional scalar arrays, not right after
  the normals. Read literally after the normals, 1,541 good records have a nonzero word there
  (the scalar flag) and 850 refused ones read zero.
- Six records in `14f9214b` and `a0ab30c8` have words 1, 96, 2 and a 3 KB tail. Not decoded.

**Claim 2, repeated IDs: holds (both).** In all 552 groups the faces sharing an ID are in different
(section, body) pairs, and no native ID repeats inside one body (prove, 9 parts). A native-only scan
of 185 parts finds 716 IDs shared across bodies in 19 parts and 0 repeated inside a body
(disprove). Prove's geometry check (display box contains the native loop's vertices, 1 µm)
passes 1,839 of 1,871 testable faces, 98.3%, under its own 99% bar. The 32 misses aren't
explained.

**Found along the way.** 4,622 records in 21 parts don't read in the usual layout at all
(disprove), so they aren't in the 13,533. 4,432 of them have no 8-byte array after the bounding
record and an id/tag block 16 bytes earlier, with zero words and a valid tag on all 4,432. The
other 190 are in 3 parts. The EXP-110 disprove check found the same two groups independently, as
SolidWorks 2025/2026 saves (4,432 faces) and a 140-byte bounding record (190 faces). The
converter reads no metadata for either. The converter's comment "56 faces in 3 corpus parts" is
corrected.
