# EXP-062 — Independent adversarial audit of EXP-059–061

Date: 2026-09-21. Audited staging commit: `511282ed3f866d78d4a3c3396a37ef57c60c1ab6`.

## Verdict

The observed Block2 relation, bounding-record layout and legacy partition wrapper survive independent checks. Several stronger semantic conclusions do not follow from the evidence. This audit qualifies those conclusions without modifying the historical experiments or parser.

| Claim | Independent result | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Block2 count equals precursor count; each word is `2*L[i]-2` | Zero deviations, 11,515 strips / 1,414 faces | 45 modern files |
| Bounding centre is midpoint; radius is corner distance; min <= max | All 1,414 pass, arithmetic errors below 1e-12 m | Extends the original 142-face check |

The fresh DisplayLists walker uses the v0.1 container decompressor, but does not use v0.2 to select baseline faces or validate their Block2 values. ## Block2: sound result, circular original selection

EXP-061's script reads raw words, but obtains face offsets and strip lengths from `v2.parseSLDPRT`. That parser already rejects invalid Block1/Block2 relationships. Raw access after that gate cannot test whether rejected candidates violate the relation.

Control: increment the first Block2 word of `Dekor.SLDPRT` at the first selected face (face offset 5590), leaving structural headers unchanged. The independent walker retains the candidate; v0.2 drops it with `Invalid Block1/Block2 lengths`. The unmodified corpus still has zero deviations under the independent walker. Therefore the numerical result survives, while the original claim of independence does not. Redundancy is established within the tested layout/corpus; retain validation on future inputs.

## Bounding record: layout upheld, origin and tightness remain open

All 1,414 independently located records have ordered bounds and pass both identities below 1e-12 m. Offsets are relative to the end of Block3; fields are little-endian float64:

| Byte offsets | Meaning supported by the checks |
|---|---|
| 12, 20, 28 | Centre |
| 36, 44, 52 | Maximum corner |
| 60, 68, 76 | Minimum corner |
| 84 | Distance from centre to a box corner |

The unknown-byte inventory in EXP-061/INV-026 is incorrect. The decoded doubles occupy **12–91 inclusive**. Offsets 32, 56, 80 and 88 are inside those doubles, not separate unknown words. The remaining unassigned ranges are **0–11 and 92–131**.

| Corpus | Faces | Mesh contained allowing 1e-9 m | Mesh contained after rounding bounds with Math.fround | Bounds equal mesh within 1e-12 m | Largest containment gap (m) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| SW2022 | 142 | 140 | 139 | 0 | 1.341104505225843e-9 |
| Original modern | 1,272 | 591 | 1,198 | 0 | 2.384185793236071e-8 |

There are 77 strictly positive containment gaps among the 142 SW2022 faces. The original 140/142 result is reproduced only when its 1e-9 m tolerance is stated. The inequalities are not exact containment. Float32 precision is magnitude-dependent; neither a universal 1 nm allowance nor rounding each bound once explains every case by itself. These small differences are compatible with floating-point/tessellation effects, but the audit does not assign every discrepancy a proven cause.

Two recorded countermodels delimit what the arithmetic can prove. Swapping the min/max triples preserves midpoint and corner-radius relations while failing ordering. Padding the observed mesh bounds by 1e-4 m and recomputing centre/radius produces an ordered, containing, non-tight box satisfying both identities. These are logical controls, not claims that the stored record was generated that way. They refute the inference from self-consistency alone to a unique, exact analytic box. The stored layout is strongly supported; tightness and provenance need external geometry comparisons.

## Post-Z field and other qualifications

The fresh binary probe locates the SW2022 boundary using the final `mesh_offset_data` declaration and checks the following Z. For SW2011 it uses the first `Z 00 02` marker: this is a corpus-specific probe, not a general record framer. The text probe reads the first `Z1 ... 2 3` sequence. One distinct raw prefix occurs in each version cohort.

The binary-minus-text field delta equals native build-log face count on all 24 modern pairs, but is zero on all 25 legacy pairs. STEP `ADVANCED_FACE` counts are recorded as a separate reference: they differ from native counts on 13/24 modern exports, so substituting them would incorrectly reject the observed modern relation.

A field changing when a hole moves disproves an invariant topology-only count, but does **not** exclude a serialized-node count: equivalent topology may serialize different geometry, control or attribute nodes. Its semantic name remains open until records can be framed and counted. Likewise, the observed modern double after this field is `1000.0000000000001`, not exactly `1000.0`; an adjacent `1e-8` value does not independently establish size-box/tolerance semantics.

EXP-060's interpretation of the adjacent 132 bytes is supported by the bounding layout. It does not establish the global absence of a fourth array or other geometry elsewhere in the stream. No complete Parasolid node decoder was validated here.

## Handoff

Use the Block2 relation and bounding offsets as corpus-supported structural knowledge. Preserve raw bounds and unknown bytes; do not advertise exact analytic extrema yet. The next discriminating checks are independent STEP/native face extrema and legacy DisplayLists record discovery (NQ-045), followed by explicit Parasolid record framing to test the field's candidate meanings. EXP-063 is the next number at this checkpoint; recheck staging before reserving it. No parser/converter/viewer implementation was changed by this audit.
