# EXP-107: blind check of the display join

Partly holds. The counts reproduce exactly, and the agent's own checks contradict only 3 of
13,375 joins, each by 8 to 22 µm. Another 33 flagged joins are in parts whose display names
edges the native body doesn't have. Most joins are confirmed by edge ids alone, and where the
geometry step chose between candidates, nothing could tell those candidates apart.

A Haiku agent ran this blind on `feature/display-ids`, with its own edge-id and closed-form
surface checks, not the branch's `geometry()` (`scripts/EXP107/`). It wrote no findings file, so
this comes from its report.

## Results (measured)

- Counts: 13,533 display faces; by id 11,483, by type 337, by geometry 1,555, ambiguous 10, none 148. `d2515121`: 2,502 of 2,512 matched.
- The agent's verdicts on the 13,375 matches: right on both checks 438; right on one check 12,895 (almost all edge ids only, since small faces have no interior vertices once boundary and seam copies are left out); wrong 36; unchecked 6.
- The 36 wrong:
  - 33 have display edge ids that don't exist in the native body at all: `0132519a` 24, `a0ab30c8` 5, `543861c9` 2, `09d353a9` 2. These are the same four parts EXP-104 found with 148 faces matching nothing. The display may be stale or from another configuration. Not resolved.
  - 3 have equal edge ids but interior vertices off the surface: `e8c40c25` raw 3924 and 3938 (spheres, 8.4 µm) and `07ff8da1` raw 1252 (torus, 22 µm). Not explained.
- Geometry-decided joins: in 787 of 1,555, two or more same-id, same-type candidates share their edge-id set, and in 493 of those an alternative passes the boundary check too (example: `d2515121` raw 2, natives 108187 and 7851, both about 4e-11 m). No check here separates those candidates.
- Control: each match paired with a different native face of the same type is rejected 13,325 of 13,343 times (99.87%). None of the controls passed both checks.
- Tests on the branch: 75 pass, 2 skipped (the OpenCascade tests, no OCP Python).
- The agent caught a bug in its own first check: vertices that the display cycle tracer drops were counted as interior. It fixed that before reporting.

## Decision

Merged into converter `dev` as one commit. The join is right wherever these checks can test it,
and wrong joins drop from 493 to 5 by EXP-104's check. The limits below stay open.

## Not checked yet

- Telling apart candidates that share id, type and edge ids in `d2515121`. They may be duplicated geometry.
- Spun, B-spline, blend and swept faces: the surface check had no closed form for them.
