## Question

After EXP-077 every storage location was known, but 7 of the 10 real parts with a solid still
exported STEP from the mesh, because the writer could not express four kinds of geometry:
INTERSECTION curves, tolerant edges, SWEPT_SURF and BLENDED_EDGE. Can each be evaluated and
written so that every part exports exact STEP? Two side questions: can the mesh be joined to the
B-rep in files without face IDs, and what is the ghost partition?

## 1. INTERSECTION curves

Each INTERSECTION belongs to exactly one edge (20/20 in distributor; every one in the corpus); its
chart runs slightly past the edge's vertices (distributor: chart end 0.288 mm, vertex 0.285 mm);
LIMIT type `H` with start = end marks a closed curve.

Method (`lib/brep/intersection.js`): walk the chart polyline from the edge's start vertex to its end
(once round for a ring), place each sample exactly on both surfaces by Newton on
`d1(x) = 0, d2(x) = 0, (x − q)·T = 0`, take the tangent as `n1 × n2`, join with cubic Hermite pieces
and split any piece whose midpoint is more than 1e-9 m from the true curve. The result is a cubic
B-spline (interior knots of multiplicity 3).

| | result |
|---|---|
| intersection edges written | 401 (Dekor 8, Gear 24, Pocket Wheel 212, chainwheel 120, distributor 20, USB BOTTOM 8, USB TOP 9) |
| worst fit deviation | 1.0e-9 m |
| independent check: 101 samples per curve against both face surfaces (148 curves in distributor, BOTTOM, chainwheel) | worst 9.3e-10 m |

A trimmed INTERSECTION, and an INTERSECTION used as a blend spine with no edge of its own, are
computed the same way (spine: from chart end to chart end).

## 2. Tolerant edges

Built from the positive fin's curve: `TRIMMED_CURVE → SP_CURVE → B_CURVE`, 2D (u,v) mapped through
the fin's face surface, with derivative `S_u u' + S_v v'`, fitted by the same Hermite procedure to
1e-9 m. 27 edges (Dekor 20, USB TOP 7), worst fit 9.9e-10 m, ends within 1e-9 m of the vertices.
Plane parametrisation is `p + u x + v (n × x)` (confirmed on EXP-077's trim points).

## 3. SWEPT_SURF: linear extrusions

All 311 (Dekor) have a rational cubic B_CURVE section with every control point at one z, and
`sweep = (0, 0, 1)`. Hypothesis: the surface is the section translated along `sweep`; `scale` only
scales the sweep parameter.

- The face's own edge curves (top and bottom B_CURVEs) coincide with the section in plan to 0.
- Display vertices: the per-face worst first looked like 0.56 mm, but the off vertices alternate
  with on-curve ones and each lies exactly on the chord between its neighbours (e.g. at
  t = 1275.5753 the chord predicts x = 0.303919, the vertex is 0.303919). They are chord points.
- Natural normal = section tangent × sweep: outward normals agree with the display normals on
  311/311 faces.

Rational control points are **homogeneous** (x·w, y·w, z·w, w): divided, Dekor's sections have
constant z (−0.01) and land on the mesh; undivided, they miss by 4.3 cm.

Written as STEP `SURFACE_OF_LINEAR_EXTRUSION` (exact).

## 4. BLENDED_EDGE: rolling-ball blends

| part | blend faces | spine | supports | range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pocket Wheel | 32 | ELLIPSE | cylinder / cylinder | (−0.008, −0.008) |
| USB hub TOP | 1 (`E`) | INTERSECTION of cylinder r 4.5 mm and torus 2 mm / 2 mm | cylinder r 2.5 mm / BLENDED_EDGE of radius 0 | (−0.002, 0.002) |

Pocket Wheel: every interior display vertex of all 32 faces is 8.0000 mm from the spine ellipse
(median and maximum 0.0080000 m; the minimum, 0.00793 m, is boundary chord points). So the face is
swept by a ball of radius |range| centred on the spine. At each spine point the ball touches each
support at the support's point nearest the centre; the face is the shorter arc between the two
contacts.

USB TOP: the radius-0 blend (`range = (0, 0)`, supports a plane and a 2 mm cylinder, spine the
circle where they meet) is the sharp edge itself. The `E` blend's spine is the intersection of its
cylinder support offset by 2 mm (r = 4.5 mm) and a 2 mm tube round that edge circle: a 2 mm ball
rolling between the cylinder and the edge.

`range[k]` is the offset of support k to the spine along the support's natural normal times its
sense (TOP: cylinder sense `−`, range −0.002, spine on the r = 4.5 mm cylinder outside the
r = 2.5 mm one).

**BLEND_BOUND** (`boundary`, `blend`): one surface of the INTERSECTION that bounds a blend face. As
an implicit surface: offset x along support k's sense-adjusted normal by `range[k]` (the would-be
ball centre), then measure that point's offset from the other support minus its `range`. With
`k = boundary − 1` the three chart points of USB TOP's INTERSECTION 879 evaluate to 0.00e+0; with
`k = boundary` they evaluate to 3.2e-3 m. **`boundary` is 1-based.**

Natural normal: away from the spine. Outward normals agree with the mesh on 32/32 Pocket Wheel
faces and the TOP face.

Written as bicubic B-splines (`lib/brep/blend.js`): global cubic interpolation of the exact
surface on a (spine × arc) grid over the spine range the face's edges cover, grid doubled until
every cell centre is within 1e-8 m. Pocket Wheel: 64 × 24 grids, worst 2.0e-9 m; at on-surface
mesh vertices the fitted and the exact distance agree to 1e-8 m. A Pocket Wheel blend is
three-sided: its two contacts meet at one end, where the arc shrinks to a point.

## 5. Trimmed curves on reversed basis curves

USB TOP has TRIMMED_CURVEs of sense `+` on CIRCLEs of sense `−`, with `parm_1 > parm_2`. The edge
runs along the basis' parameter only when both senses agree; using the trimmed curve's sense alone
sent two edges the long way round (round trip failed: "no STEP edge on it"; OpenCascade: open
shell). With the product rule both pass. No controlled model has a `−` basis under a trimmed curve.

## 6. Volume integrator changes

- A ring edge on a non-periodic curve (a closed intersection) runs over the curve's own range, not
  2π (distributor had read −6e15 mm³).
- Boundary integrals are split at the knots of B-spline edge curves; inner integrals use at least
  one 10-point Gauss rule per surface knot span (exact for non-rational bicubics).
- B-spline faces: (u,v) inverted edge by edge from each edge's middle with warm starts and lightly
  damped Newton; du/dt from the exact tangent where the surface is regular, by finite differences
  only where it degenerates; a straight parameter-space segment closes the loop where consecutive
  edges meet at one 3D point but different (u,v). Without it the three-sided blend faces integrated
  to 0.
- Controlled corpus after the changes: ours vs SolidWorks' STEP worst 1.4e-12 → back to the EXP-076
  level once derivatives are exact where regular (C16: 2.4e-16 and 0).

## The ghost partition

`Config-0-GhostPartition`: one `(partition)` section (EXP-074). Survey of the 73 files:

| content | files |
|---|---|
| one `body_type` 2 body: one vertex at (0,0,0), no edges or faces, INT attribute 10001 | 61 (distributor: six such bodies, 10001–10006) |
| no ghost partition (but a partition) | 5: C02 ×3, PTC GE8080-8, cube.SLDPRT |
| wire and sheet bodies with geometry, `GHOST_REF_BODY_ID_2001` | 4 |
| no partitions at all | 3 (pre-2011) |

The four with geometry:

- C16, both eras: bodies of type 3, 3, 2, 2. Wires: 4 LINEs (a 10 mm square at y = 0) and a CIRCLE
  (r 4 mm at y = 0.01): the loft's two profile sketches. Sheets: two planes, at y = 0 and y = 0.01.
  Every wire edge lies on a part edge (0 m); both planes are part planes (0 m). One sheet face
  shares its node_id (23) with the part's end cap.
- Helical Bevel Gear: types 2, 3, 3, 3, 2, 2, 2. Three planar sheets and 96 circular wire edges.
  Wire ends lie on part edges (4.2e-17 m); two planes are part planes, the third is 0.66 mm from any.
- Pocket Wheel: types 3, 2, 2. A conical sheet 29 mm from any part cone, and wires on part edges.

`body_type` 2 bodies have only edges and vertices, type 3 bodies one open face each: wire and sheet
bodies. The ghost partition holds feature input geometry (profile sketches, section planes,
reference surfaces) and never the solid. Which feature each body belongs to, and what the default
single vertex stands for, are open (NQ-050).

## What this establishes

- Every real part with a solid in the corpus exports exact STEP: valid single solids, volumes
  matching OpenCascade within each part's defined precision. Only intersection curves (1e-9 m) and
  rolling-ball blends (1e-8 m) are approximated, because STEP has no exact form for them.
- SWEPT_SURF here is a linear extrusion; rational control points are homogeneous; the blend natural
  normal points away from the spine; BLEND_BOUND `boundary` is 1-based; a trimmed curve's direction
  is the product of its and its basis' senses.
- Every off-surface display boundary vertex is a chord point.
- The mesh joins the B-rep in every version with an edge table, by edge IDs.

## Limits

- One `E`-type blend and one radius-0 support; extrusions only along +z; no SWEPT_SURF with a
  non-planar section; no tolerant edge on a cone or sphere (only plane, cylinder, torus, B-spline
  checked).
- Volumes of real parts are compared with OpenCascade, not SolidWorks (no reference values).
- 231 interior and 11 boundary display vertices are unexplained.
