# EXP-122: NQ-067's two SP_CURVE vertices far off their curves

2026-11-26. Two different causes.

- **`f0b9a118` edge 152 (4.49e-4 m) is a converter error.** The edge is a thread. The fitter finds
  each end's parameter by projecting the vertex onto the SP_CURVE, and the projection's Newton
  starts from the nearest of 64 samples, about 4.5 per coil. For vertex 0 it settled a coil early.
  The file's own trim puts the vertex on the curve to 7.4e-19 m. With the trim seeding the
  projection, the edge fits to 9.95e-9 m (converter change below, sweep pending).
- **`0739c559` edge 6075 (1.57e-4 m) isn't settled.** The edge's last stretch runs past the end of
  the B-surface its offset is built on (3e-4 in v, 0.18 mm on the basis). The offset is 4.826 mm towards the centre of a basis that
  curves with a radius of 4.99 mm across u, so the offset is nearly degenerate there (radius
  0.16 mm). Where the curve's end lands depends on how the basis is extended past its range: our
  linear extension puts it 1.59e-4 m from the vertex, the end span's polynomial 8.26e-5 m. The
  vertex tolerance is 4.82e-5 m, so neither is right, and the file doesn't say which extension
  Parasolid uses.

Scripts: `scripts/EXP122/`, outputs next to them.

The vertex is on all three of its faces to 9e-19 m (`far-f0b9a118.out`). Fitting with the trim's
parameters in place of the projection: 9.95e-9 m (`seed-f0b9a118-152.out`).

## 0739c559 edge 6075

The edge runs between a cylinder (6272) and an offset (6288) of B-surface 6328 at 4.826 mm. Its
curve is an SP_CURVE on the offset along u = 0, from v = 0.008945 (the end of the range) to
v = 0.007040. The basis's v range starts at 0.007338, so the last stretch, t 0.82 to 0.787, is on
the extension (`offcyl-0739c559-6075.out`):

| t | v | from the cylinder | from the cylinder fin |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 0.947 | 0.008469 | 6.2e-7 m | 1.6e-6 m |
| 0.830 | 0.007425 | 7.9e-8 m | 3.5e-6 m |
| 0.808 | 0.007231 (extension) | 1.4e-7 m | 3.9e-5 m |
| 0.787 | 0.007040 (extension) | 4.5e-7 m | 1.0e-4 m |

While v is in range the two fins agree within 3.5e-6 m, inside the edge tolerance of 1e-5. The
gap opens only past v0.

The basis is rational, degree 2 across u: an arc of radius 4.99 mm at v0 and 5.33 mm at the other
end, with its centre on the side the offset goes (`ru.out`). The offset across u is therefore an
arc of radius 0.16 mm at v0, and its u = 0 and u = 1 edges are 1.05e-4 m apart there, against 3.2 mm
on the basis (`seam.out`). Vertex 1's foot on our offset is u = 1, v = 0.007122, also on the
extension (`ext.out`).

The curve's end, evaluated with two extensions of the basis past v0 (`poly.out`):

| | linear (the converter) | end span's polynomial |
|---|---:|---:|
| inside the range, against the surface | same | 0 m on the basis, 8e-10 m on the offset (control) |
| curve end to vertex 1 | 1.59e-4 m | 8.26e-5 m |
| nearest point of the curve to vertex 1 | 1.57e-4 m | 5.09e-5 m |
| from the cylinder fin at t 0.800 | 6.39e-5 m | 5.10e-6 m |
| from the cylinder fin at the end | 1.02e-4 m | 6.48e-5 m |

The polynomial is closer, but no extension tried puts the end within 4.82e-5 m of the vertex, so
neither result is a measurement of Parasolid's extension. Trying more extensions until one fits
would be tuning the check. A SolidWorks export of this part (STEP or `.x_t`) would show where
Parasolid puts the edge.

## Across the online sample

`corpus.js` takes every edge whose curve is a trimmed SP_CURVE with two vertices (2,127 edges in
136 parts; 5 files have no Parasolid partition) and compares each end two ways: the converter's
projection, and the trim's own end (`corpus.jsonl`, `corpus.summary`).

| | edges |
|---|---:|
| both within 1e-8 m | 1,956 |
| projection off, trim on the vertex | 1 (`f0b9a118` 152) |
| trim at least as far off as the projection, within tolerance | 169 (73 under 1e-7 m, 62 under 1e-6, 28 under 1e-5, 6 under 1e-4) |
| trim outside its tolerance | 1 (`0739c559` 6075, as far off as the projection) |

So the trim can't replace the projection: on 169 edges its stored ends are a little further from
the vertices. It can seed it.

## The change

`curveProject` takes an optional seed and runs a second Newton from it, keeping the nearer foot.
`spcurveEdge` seeds each end with the trim's parameter (from the edge's curve or a fin over the same
2D curve), the trim end nearer a vertex going to that vertex. An end can only move nearer its
vertex.
