# EXP-074 — Display layer joined to the native B-rep

Captured 2026-09-29 UTC. Base: staging `4cd53b4`. Run `node knowledge/evidence/scripts/EXP074/display-native-join.js --write`. [Source](scripts/EXP074/display-native-join.js) and [raw results](scripts/EXP074/RESULTS.json) record every count, crosstab cell, per-face residual for off-surface faces, section offsets and the notes quoted below.

## Why this test

Two readers in this repository were built independently of each other. `parser/v0.3` reads DisplayLists (tessellation plus per-face metadata). Its layout was fixed from file bytes in v0.4.x, long before any Parasolid record was decoded. EXP-071's typed reader, fed by EXP-072's extraction, decodes the native `Config-0-Partition` B-rep. Neither consults the other. If both are right, three joins must hold that neither reader was built to make true. EXP-074 checks all three over the whole corpus: 24 SW2022, 25 SW2011 and 24 original files.

Surface residuals are coded from the typed field names alone (for example PLANE `pvec normal`, CYLINDER `pvec axis radius`, CONE `… sin_half_angle cos_half_angle`, TORUS `centre axis major_radius minor_radius`). Nothing is reused from EXP-071's audit, and nothing is cited from outside this repository. Display vertices are float32. A vertex is on-surface when |residual| ≤ 1e-6 m.

## D. Where the off-surface vertices are

228 faces have at least one vertex off their surface (6 SW2022, 222 original). By file: C20/C21 6, Pocket Wheel 176, USB TOP 17, Helical Bevel Gear 14, Dekor 8, distributor 6, USB BOTTOM 1. The off vertices were localised, not dismissed. For each one, the test asks two things:
- Is it on a display boundary (a strip edge carrying a native edge ID in Block1)?
- Does it lie within 1e-7 m of the straight segment between two of its 16 nearest on-surface vertices of the same face?

| corpus | off vertices | on boundary (by native curve) | interior | on a chord | not on a chord | max residual |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SW2022 | 158 | 156 (CIRCLE 138, ELLIPSE 18) | 2 | **158/158** | 0 | 9.0e-6 |
| original | 3461 | 3353 (CIRCLE 1270, INTERSECTION 1089, ELLIPSE 422, edge with no curve 309, TRIMMED_CURVE 263) | 108 | 3147 (78 of them interior) | **314** | 1.2e-4 |

Chord parameter buckets: SW2022 had 1/3: 41, 1/4: 21, 1/2: 11, other: 85; the C20 chord distance reaches 1.52e-11 at t = 1/3 or 2/3. The original corpus had 1/2: 300, 1/4: 87, 1/3: 58, other: 2702.

Interpretation, bounded to what was tested: the off-surface vertices are overwhelmingly boundary points of the display polyline that lie on straight chords between on-surface or on-curve samples. They are not re-projected onto the surface. A shared edge polyline therefore sits off both adjacent surfaces. **314 original vertices are neither on a chord nor explained. They are published as open, not as noise.**

**Falsified: declared tolerance as the explanation.**
- 216 of the 228 off-surface faces declare no tolerance on any bounding edge or vertex (`tolerance` null throughout).
- The 12 faces that do declare one (5.05e-7 or 1e-5) have only 83 off vertices within it, out of 3619 off vertices in total.
- The offsets reach 1.2e-4, so declared tolerance does not explain them.

## E. Partition stream grammar

Every partition section has this layout:

```text
u32le size | GUID 231dd571da8148a2a85898b21b89ef99 | u32le inflated | u32le compressed | zlib[compressed] | 8 bytes
size = compressed + 32
```

| check | result |
|---|---|
| trailing 8 bytes are zero | **242/242** sections |
| `Config-N-Partition` = "TRANSMIT FILE (partition)" then "TRANSMIT FILE (deltas)", chaining exactly to stream end, 0-byte prefix | **70/70** |
| `Config-N-GhostPartition` = one "(partition)" section, exact chain | **65/65** |

The GUID also heads valid sections outside the Partition streams:

- **`Config-0-FeatureBodies/LocalBodies`** (PTC GE8080-8 only). There is a 64-byte prefix before the section: `01000000`, then `fffeff13` followed by UTF-16LE `PTC GE8080-8.stp<1>`, then fields including `0xf535`, not yet decoded. It holds one plain "TRANSMIT FILE" section with a 4334-node BODY and **126 faces**. **Joined, the 126 faces satisfy §A–§C with every other original file.** This answers EXP-073's counterexample: PTC's primary partition is empty because its body is an imported local body stored here.
- **`Config-N-ResolvedFeatures`** holds 36 valid sections in 14 files. Every one is plain, a **1-face PLANE body** (`body_type` field value 3) of 20–169 nodes. Counts per file:
  - C18 split line: 1 in each era
  - C04/C05/C06/C11: 1 each; C07/C08: 2 each
  - Dekor: 1; Pocket Wheel: 2; Helical Bevel Gear: 3; distributor: 7
  - USB BOTTOM: 3; USB TOP: 10. BOTTOM's three node counts (54, 86, 28) are TOP's first three.

  In the controlled cubes the count follows the number of cuts or split lines. That is an observation, not a decoded role (NQ-048).

chainwheel, plate4 and SW2000-s01 have no `Config-0-Partition` and are not read, as in EXP-073.

## Independence and limits

This is a clean-room result. Every number here comes from our corpus through code in this repository; no external project or private-repository finding is cited or used. Display vertices are float32, so the 1e-6 on-surface threshold is roughly two orders above float32 rounding at part scale. B-surface, swept and blended surfaces are counted but not geometrically evaluated. SW2011 display metadata remains undecoded.
