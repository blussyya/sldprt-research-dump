# EXP-114: the four cone faces with normals 62 degrees off (NQ-073)

2026-11-24. No converter error. The four faces in "distributor main boss rev a" are drill-point cones
(half-angle 59.000 degrees, a 118 degree drill), and the 62 degree mismatches are all at the apex. A
cone has no single normal at its apex; each copy of the apex vertex in the mesh carries the normal of
its own triangle's generator, and 180 − 2 × 59 = 62. The cones are right: every display vertex lies
on the converter's cone within 5.8e-10 m.

Blind checks (2026-11-24, `scripts/EXP113/prove/`, `disprove/`): holds. Scripts: `scripts/EXP114/cones.js`, `apex.js`, `extent.js`, outputs
