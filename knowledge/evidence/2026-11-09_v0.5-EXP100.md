# EXP-100: blind check of tags 4008 (offset) and 4010 (spun)

The names hold. Every 4010 face that joins is a spun surface, and the one 4008 face is an offset
surface. The display mesh's vertices sit on the joined surface about as closely as they do on the
cylinder and cone controls. A distance test at 1e-8 m can't tell spun from cylinder here, because
the controls miss that bar too.

A Haiku agent ran this blind, with its own scripts (`scripts/EXP100/`, `findings.md`). It didn't
see EXP-097's scripts or write-up. I recounted the tags myself: 286 + 4 faces of 4010, 1 of 4008.

## Results (measured)

- Normals lie in the meridian plane of the native axis on 248 of 284 and 3 of 4 spun faces (bar 1e-5). The other 36 are off by up to about 2 degrees. Not explained.
- Control: 4002 and 4003 faces join as cylinder and cone (8cd79ff5 42/42 and 6/6, 2f121983 51/51 and 1/1). Planes and spheres fit at 1e-9 m, so the frame is right.
- Cylinder and cone vertices sit 1e-6 to 3e-4 m off, mean 0.3% of the radius. EXP-099's agent saw the same order on its matched curved faces. Not explained yet, and worth its own look.

## What the claim doesn't cover

- 4010 records carry no axis in their 8 parameters: all 8 are 0 on 290 of 290. The 4002 control carries its radius at index 6.
- The display `direction` isn't the axis: parallel on 260 faces, perpendicular on 24 in `a4ecc6f5`, and perpendicular on 4 of 4 in `8cd79ff5`.
- Wrong-type joins (a 4002 and a 4003 joined to a plane, a 4001 to a cylinder) all sit on repeated IDs. EXP-099 explains those.
