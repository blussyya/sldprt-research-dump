# EXP-102: blind check of the EXP-098 tie-breaker

Partly held, and now fixed. The tie-breaker picked the right cube in 296 of 306 ordered runs. It
picked wrong for a plain cube with a split line (C18): its display fits a filleted cube's planes
(C03) exactly, so the tie went to whichever candidate came first. The converter now matches each
display face to a B-rep face of its own (`dev` 705b69b), and C18 picks itself in both orders.

A Haiku agent ran this blind, with its own two-sided scorer (OpenCascade, display against the
trimmed B-rep and back). It didn't see EXP-098's scripts. Scripts and outputs are in
`scripts/EXP102/`. It couldn't write a findings file, so this section comes from its report. I
reproduced the C18 miss and tested the fix myself.

## What failed (measured)

- C18's display against each of the 7-face box-fit candidates: residual 2.2e-10 m, angle 0, C03 included. `meshResidual` checked display vertices against unbounded surfaces, and several display faces could share one surface. Both halves of C18's split face sat on one of C03's planes.
- The agent's scorer gives C03 a 0.414 mm gap for C18's display (√2 − 1 mm) and C18 itself 3.9e-7 mm.
- Order: reversing the candidates changed the pick in 14 of 306 keys. All 14 were ties: 8 C18 keys and 6 identical-geometry twins (C00/C22, C13/C25, C04/C26). The code keeps the first tied candidate. The real caller passes configurations in ascending ID order, so "lowest ID" holds there.
- Noise: uniform synthetic noise of 1e-8 m on the display flips chamfer C09 to fillet C03 on 1 of 5 seeds, and 2e-8 m on 5 of 5. The chamfer's position residual (4.3e-10 m) sits under the 1e-8 m bar, and noise pushes it over, so the rule compares positions instead of angles. Float32 rounding at 10 mm is about 5e-10 m, 20 times below the flip. Not tested on real rounding.

## What held (measured)

- Fillet against chamfer is decided by normal angle: 0.785 rad against under 4e-7 rad.
- Holes are decided by position, with the smallest correct margin 5e-4 m (C26 against C11).
- The agent's scorer picks the same as the converter on every target except C18. Its margins over the runner-up are 34 to 177 times its own floor.
- Controls: a 0.01 mm display shift reads as 0.01 mm. The known gaps come out as geometry predicts: C03/C09 0.293 mm (1 − 1/√2), C04/C05 1.00 mm.

## The fix (2026-11-09, measured)

`meshResidual` now takes the smallest worst distance any one-to-one assignment of display faces
to B-rep faces reaches, then the smallest worst angle within it (`src/parasolid/select.js`).

- Results: C18 picks itself in both orders, with a new test that fails on the old code. Across 44 runs (22 SW2011 cubes, both orders), the only other picks are the 6 identical twins.
- Tests: 68 pass (the new one included), 2 skipped.
- Corpus: the nine multi-configuration parts pick the same configuration as before (268, 21, 0, 0, 0, 0, lowest 0, Header2 7, Header2 3).
- Speed: `118a3cb0` takes 61 s, against 66 s with the old code, so it's no slower. The time goes on the box test over 25 configurations.
