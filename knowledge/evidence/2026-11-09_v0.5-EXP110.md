# EXP-110: the display bounds record's 40-byte tail

Partly resolved. The tail (+92..+131) is not five flags and a vector. Two of its words are
decoded exactly, the "directional variant" is a normal cone whose axis follows the face's
outward normal, and the rest is mostly stale memory. The scalar is a cone cosine, but I could not
reproduce its exact value from the stored normals. That part stays open.

Run on converter `dev` (`src/display.js`, `src/brep/display-join.js`, joined with
`src/brep/native.js`) over 183 modern parts, 13,533 faces with a bounds record and an id block.
Scripts and outputs: `scripts/EXP110/`. No blind prove or disprove agent was run (no subagent tool
in this session), so nothing here is independently checked yet.

## Word map (measured)

Offsets are from the start of the 132-byte record. `rows.jsonl` holds every record's tail bytes,
display metadata and joined native surface (`extract.js`).

| offset | what it is | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| +92 | 1 on a plane, 0 on every other surface | 6,386 of 6,386 plane tags are 1, 0 of 7,147 others. By native type: 6,341 of 6,341 planes, 0 non-planes |
| +96 | 0 or 1; mostly a per-file property, meaning not found | see below |
| +100 | not a face property: `999999` in 5,367 records, otherwise a 32-bit value that looks like a memory address | see below |
| +104 | 1 when the cone is stored | with +108, below |
| +108 | 1 by default; 0 in files that store cones when a face has no cone | with +104, below |
| +112..+127 | four floats (scalar, vector) when +104 and +108 are 1; otherwise unexplained | below |
| +128 | 1 | 13,533 of 13,533 |

EXP-092 listed +128 as one of five binary words. It's a constant, so the real flags are +92, +96,
+104 and +108. EXP-092's counts differ from these by 56 records and 12 vectors. The 12 are faces
with surface tag 4980815, which the old reader accepted and `dev` rejects (tags must be 4001 to
4010). The 56 are probably the faces EXP-099 found with no id block; not checked.

## The directional variant is a normal cone

When +104 and +108 are both 1, +112 is a float `s` and +116..+124 a unit vector `v`. It occurs on
2,974 records: cylinders 2,406, B-spline 242, spun 164, cones 126, tori 24, spheres 6, blends 5,
swept 1. Planes never have it.

- `v` follows the face's outward normal. On cylinders joined to a native cylinder (2,398):
  - |v . native axis| is at most 2.82e-8 on all 2,398. EXP-097 could only check 740 of them.
    This check alone proves little, since any radial direction passes.
  - The sign is the test. With the native face sense the same as the surface sense, `v` points from
    the axis to the bounds box centre within 1 degree on 1,493 of 1,735, and never points the
    opposite way. With the senses opposed it points the opposite way within 1 degree on 540 of
    663, and never the same way (`cyl-sense.out.txt`). A sign-flipped `v` fails all of those.
  - The rest (365) are further than 1 degree off the box-centre direction. Those are wide
    arcs, where the box centre isn't the arc middle. Not individually explained.
- Against the stored vertex normals, `v` is within 0.52 degrees of their mean on the median cylinder
  (95th percentile 5.6, max 17.0). Other surfaces: medians 0.5 to 6.7 degrees, max 18.2
  (`native-check.out.txt`).
- On cones, |v . axis| is not the cone's sin(half angle) (max difference 0.179 over 126).
  On tori |v . axis| runs 0.186 to 0.721. So `v` is not a surface axis; it is a direction of the
  face's normals.
- Of EXP-097's 396 "points to the box centre", this explains them: those are the outward cases.

`s` is a cone cosine, but not exactly the cosine of the largest normal angle:

- `s` ranges 0.70837 to 0.99999. It never drops below 1/sqrt(2) (0.70711): the smallest of 2,974
  is 0.70837, and 75 are under 0.72.
- Measured against the stored vertex normals, `s` is never lower than the cosine of the largest
  angle between a normal and `v` (0 of 2,974 are more than 1e-5 below it). It equals it within
  1e-5 on 855 (cylinders 826, cones 28, swept 1), and is higher on 2,119, by up to 0.111. So the
  stored cone is tighter than the stored normals on 2,119 faces, and some vertex normals fall outside it.
- It is exact when the face has two distinct normals: 832 of 2,398 cylinders match the angular
  half-span of the display vertices within 1e-4 rad, and these have 4 to 11 vertices.
  With more distinct normals the cone is narrower than the arc. A 45 degree fillet tessellated at 15 degree
  steps has half-span 45.000 and `acos(s)` 40.455 in every part tried (4 files), with `v` 1.095 degrees from
  the arc middle. The same shortfall in `acos(s)` grows with the span (0.12 degrees at 12.6 degrees,
  0.79 at 24.1, 4.5 at 45) and depends on the tessellation, not only on the span (arcs of
  43.5 degrees with 20 vertices and 43.3 with 28 differ by 3 degrees).

## When the cone is stored

- **A file-level switch plus a width limit.** In 48 files with cylinders under 45 degrees half-span, none
  mixes cone and no-cone narrow cylinders (0 mixed). Only 14 of the 48 store cones. In the files
  that store them, every cylinder under 45 degrees has one (2,187 of 2,187), and cylinders over about 50 degrees
  half-span (arc measured on the display vertices around the native axis) never do: the widest with a
  cone is 50.4 degrees, and 14 of 15 at 50 to 60 degrees have none (`cyl-thresh.js`).
- Which files store cones is not explained. They span versions 11000 to 17000 (18 files, includes
  `C03`/`C12` made on SW2022), but so do files that don't.
- Threshold: in the cone-storing files, non-plane faces without a cone (state 0,0) have a normal
  spread (largest angle to the mean normal) of at least 45.24 degrees (814 faces with a usable mean; one more has none). Faces with a cone
  all have `acos(s)` of at most 45 degrees. Consistent with "store only when the cone half-angle is
  under 45 degrees". The spread of cone faces measured the same way reaches 64.19, because the
  stored cone is tighter than the normals (above), so the 45 degree figure is on the stored `s`, not on
  the spread. The no-cone side was not measured with `s`.
- Control cases (SW2022, known geometry): the 90 degree fillet (C03, C12) has a cone. Full holes
  (C04 to C08, C11), half cylinder (C19), crossed cylinders (C20), cone (C13), sphere (C14), quarter
  torus (C17) and cylinder fillet (C21) have none.

## The other states

Each non-plane face is in one of three states of (+104, +108); planes are always (0, 1).

| state | rows | where |
|---|---:|---|
| (1,1) cone stored | 2,974 | non-planes in files that store cones |
| (0,1) default | 3,323 non-planes, 6,386 planes | non-planes only in files without cones; planes everywhere, 2,103 of them in cone files |
| (0,0) none | 850 non-planes | 815 in files that store cones, 35 in others |

`byfile.js` and the tables in `xtab.js` give the counts. The 35 elsewhere are in C04 to C21 controls,
`PTC GE8080-8`, `Profilringe` and one face of `14f9214b_11`. They are all wide faces (full holes,
sphere, cone), so they may be cone-storing files that have no narrow face. That can't be told
from this data.

What decides (0,0) versus (0,1) among non-planes is not found. In the C-series a full torus
(C15) and the loft (C16) are (0,1), while a full sphere (C14), full cone (C13), quarter torus (C17)
are (0,0). Face counts of loops, edges and scalar arrays didn't separate them.

## The other words

- **+100.** It is `999999` on 5,367 records: all 290 spun faces, 70% of B-spline, 57% of cylinder, 25% of plane faces, none of the tori, spheres, blends or most swept faces.
  The rest are 8,159 distinct values. On the C-series cubes, consecutive faces differ by exactly
  0x40000 and the low 16 bits are `0x0068`, `0x006a`, `0x0069`, and so on. That looks like the
  low half of a heap pointer, not a surface property. It correlates with nothing found: not state, tag or
  file version. Not checked: whether `999999` and the pointer-like values are the same field with two
  initialisers (two parts of the code), or two different ones. Pointer reading is a guess.
- **+112..+127 when there is no cone.** Not floats. Sampled values repeat across unrelated files:
  `1c00b800 0c040700`, `0a007002 70ab0000 38000000`, `ffffffff`, `0000f0bf`. That is the signature of
  uninitialised stack or struct memory, a guess; all four words zero in 4,472 of the 10,559 records with no cone. The 4-byte-aligned double
  scan (`dbl.js`) finds plausible values everywhere, including the vector state where there are none,
  so it can't tell structure from noise. Not decoded.
- **+96.** 1 on 12,030 and 0 on 1,503. It is constant within a file in 112 of 115 files, and the zeros
  are in 4 files (`361e7dbb_101_HOUSING` all 262, `28398415_Raspberry Pi 4` 259 of 267,
  `a4ecc6f5_PP-2074-08` 946 of 961, `Dekor` 36 of 375), in versions 11000, 12000, 14000, 17000. It
  isn't the body (HOUSING has one body), not the scalar-array flag, not the file's save version.
  Open.

## Checked and ruled out

- `s` and `v` are not built from the display triangle normals:
  - Flat triangle normals, area-weighted sum for `v`: matches on 559 of 2,974 (within 1e-6).
  - Normals from the sum of each triangle's three vertex normals: 523 to 564, and `s` matches on 0 within 1e-6
    (about 100 within 1e-5).
  - Minimum dot of the flat normals with the stored `v` reproduces `s` within 1e-5 on 89.
  - For the 15 degree fillet the triangle normals are symmetric (7.5 to 82.5 degrees), while `v` is 1.1 degrees off.
- `v` is not the mean of vertex normals (that is 40 degrees on the 15 degree fillet, `v` is 43.9).
- `s` is not the cosine of the largest stored-normal angle (above), and not the mean resultant length
  (for two-normal faces that would equal the cosine, but it fails the three-normal ones; checked by hand on
  the 15 degree fillet, 0.861 against 0.761).
- `v` is not a surface axis (cones, tori above).
- The "five binary words" reading from EXP-092 is wrong as stated; +128 is constant.

## Not checked yet

- The exact rule for `s` and `v` on tessellations with three or more distinct normals. Candidates left:
  a cone built from sampled surface normals instead of the stored ones, or an incremental merge.
- Why 14 of 48 files store cones, and what +96 and the (0,0) versus (0,1) choice depend on. A file
  property, probably set when the display is built; no save-option comparison was available.
- The 51 axis disagreements from EXP-097 (display axis against native axis) were not revisited.
- Whether the narrow-face limit is 45 degrees on `s`: the (0,0) faces have no `s` to test.
- Legacy files (39) have no decoded tail here.
- Blind prove and disprove agents.

## Scripts (`scripts/EXP110/`)

- `extract.js` writes `rows.jsonl` (all 13,533 records) and `extract.log.json`. `filever.js` adds
  file versions (`filever.json`).
- `states.js`, `xtab.js`, `byfile.js`, `w100.js`, `peek.js`, `dbl.js`: word, state and file-level
  tables.
- `native-check.js` (`.out.txt`, `.json`), `cyl-sense.js` (`.out.txt`): `v` against native axis and
  sense, flag checks.
- `cone-test.js`, `tri-cone.js`, `vn-tri.js`, `cyl-span.js`, `signed.js`, `small.js`, `c03.js`,
  `c03b.js`: tests of `s` and `v` against the normals and triangles.
- `cyl-thresh.js`, `spread.js`: when the cone is stored.
- The `.json` files beside them are the outputs, so none of the counts above need a rerun.
  Controls: the sign-flip in `cyl-sense.js` (0 opposite matches), and the `C`-series
  parts with known geometry.
