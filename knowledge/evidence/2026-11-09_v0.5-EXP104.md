# EXP-104: joining display faces to native faces across bodies and sections

The converter now keeps each face's section-local node id. With that, display faces join to the
right native face in 13,375 of 13,533 cases (98.8%), against 9,648 before. Repeated ids are
settled by surface type, then by geometry. It was made on converter branch `feature/display-ids`, then merged into `dev` after its blind
check ([EXP-107](2026-11-09_v0.5-EXP107.md)).

A Sonnet agent made the change and measured it (`scripts/EXP104/`, `findings.md`). I reproduced
the headline counts on its branch: `d2515121` 2,502 of 2,512 matched, 10 ambiguous; `a4ecc6f5`
961 of 961; `2f121983` 155 of 155.

## What changed

- `merge()` (`src/parasolid/partition.js`) keeps `section_node_id` and `merge_section` on every node. `node_id` still shifts, because the model builder needs unique ids.
- Native faces and edges get a `displayId`, and faces get their `body`.
- The join is `src/brep/display-join.js`, used by `src/inspect.js` and `tools/validate.js`, the only two `rawId` joins in the code. Order: id; if several faces share it, surface type by tag; then geometry; else ambiguous.

## Coverage (measured, 115 modern parts, 13,533 display faces with an id block)

| | dev a256dba | branch |
|---|---:|---:|
| matched | 9,648 | 13,375 |
| ambiguous | not detected | 10 (all `d2515121`) |
| no native face | 3,885 | 148 |
| fails the geometric check | 493 | 5 |
| edge ids disagree | 709 | 33 |

- Of the matches, 11,483 are by id alone, 337 by surface type and 1,555 by geometry.
- Control: a shifted id (rawId + 1) joined 666 faces, and 660 of those fail the geometric check and all 666 fail the edge-id check. Pairing each display face with a different native face of the same type fails 98.2% (geometric) and 99.9% (edge ids).
- Display order within a body doesn't follow native face order (4,598 descents), so the join doesn't use order.
- Tests on the branch: 75 pass, 2 skipped (7 new).

The geometric check changed once. The first version wanted every display vertex within 1e-5 m,
and flagged right joins on cylinders whose boundary vertices sit up to 1.2e-4 m off, the effect
EXP-105 explains. It now wants at least half the vertices within 1e-5 m. The 1e-5 m bar didn't
move, and both runs are kept (`measure.v1-maxres.*`). The edge-id check is independent of that
rule and agrees.

## EXP-091's 941 of 1,545

This couldn't be reproduced. The same count gives 200 of 1,541 faces on `dev`, and 880 of 1,484
with the dump's old `package/` copy, so EXP-097's number came from an older reader. The
join fixes 188 of `dev`'s 200 and moves 16 faces `dev` joined wrongly onto the right one.
