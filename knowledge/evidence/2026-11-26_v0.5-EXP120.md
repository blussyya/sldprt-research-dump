# EXP-120: 3c4752f1's two plane × blend edges over 1e-8 m

2026-11-26. The curves are right, and the over-bar number doesn't measure them. Edges 1655 and 1601
report 2.53e-8 and 2.03e-8 m. Both run from a vertex where the blend touches the plane (normals
7.4e-6 degrees apart) to one where the surfaces cross at 32 degrees. From 0.1 µm away from the
touching vertex onwards, the exact curve is determined to 3e-13 m and the fitted curve is within
9.8e-11 m of it. Within 30 nm of that vertex the two surfaces don't determine the curve: exact points
corrected from guesses 1e-7 m apart land up to 2.1e-7 m apart. The fit's own check samples that
stretch, so its error there is the reference's spread, not the curve's. This is the same kind of
limit as head_back's T-limit blends (NQ-065). No converter change.

Scripts: `scripts/EXP120/` (`touch.js`, `determined.js`), outputs next to them.

## Measured

`touch.js` resolves the part (converter `main` 894360c) and samples each fitted curve at 2,001
points. At each, the converter's own corrector finds the exact intersection point in the plane
normal to the curve.

| edge | surfaces | length | reported fit error | samples over 1e-8 m | largest elsewhere |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 1655 | plane 1657 × blend 1664 | 1.5454 mm | 2.53e-8 m | 1, the touching vertex itself (9.4e-8 m) | 3.14e-11 m |
| 1601 | blend 1603 × plane 1604 | 1.5454 mm | 2.03e-8 m | 1, the touching vertex itself (6.3e-8 m) | 3.14e-11 m |

The vertices are on both surfaces: 1.4e-18 m off the planes, 8.4e-13 to 9.5e-13 m off the blends.
The blends are constant-radius rolling-ball blends, measured through their spines.

`determined.js` asks how well the exact curve is fixed at a distance from each end. It moves the
guess 1e-7 m sideways in four directions and corrects each onto the curve:

| distance from the touching end | normals apart | spread of the four exact points (1655 / 1601) | fit to the furthest (1655 / 1601) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1e-8 m | 2.7e-4 / 2.3e-4 degrees | 2.1e-7 / 8.6e-8 m | 1.3e-7 / 1.2e-7 m |
| 3e-8 m | 4.4e-4 / 5.8e-4 degrees | 1.9e-7 / 8.7e-8 m | 1.1e-7 / 5.3e-8 m |
| 1e-7 m | 1.7e-3 degrees | 2.7e-13 / 2.5e-13 m | 6.9e-11 / 9.8e-11 m |
| 1e-6 m | 2.0e-2 degrees | 2.4e-15 / 1.6e-14 m | 2.0e-11 / 2.8e-11 m |
| 1e-4 m | 2.0 degrees | under 3e-16 m | 1.5e-11 m |

Control, the other end of each edge (surfaces 32 degrees apart): spread under 2e-17 m and fit
within 1.8e-12 m at every distance from 1e-8 m to 1e-4 m.

## Why

Where two surfaces touch, a small error in either surface's distance moves the common point
sideways by that error divided by the angle between the normals. The angle grows from 0 at the
vertex, about 2 degrees per 0.1 mm here, so the curve becomes determined about 0.1 µm out. The fit
pins its end to the vertex, which lies on both surfaces to 1e-12 m. The fit check's samples in the
last interval fall inside the undetermined stretch and score the corrector's spread.

## What follows

- `3c4752f1`'s exact STEP is as good as its other edges wherever the file determines the curve.
  The reported 2.53e-8 m is a limit of the check at a touching end.
- The converter could report such a stretch instead of scoring it: skip a sample whose exact point
  moves by more than the bar between two seeds, and report the skipped length per edge. That
  changes what the check measures, so it isn't done here. It would need a blind check and the
  sweep, and it would apply to NQ-065's blend surfaces as well.
