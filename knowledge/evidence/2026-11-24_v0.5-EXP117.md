# EXP-117: chainwheel's faces without boundary cycles (NQ-072 remainder)

2026-11-24. A reader error, now fixed. In pre-2011 single-triangle strips with control word 0, the
edge ids come in the order (v,v+2), (v,v+1), (v+1,v+2): the first two are swapped against a
control-1 strip. Read in the usual order, 274 boundary edges of chainwheel lost their ids and 141 of
its 189 faces had no boundary cycles. Swapped, all 189 close, and their loops equal the native
B-rep's loops on 189 of 189 (blind disprove check). Converter commit 894360c.

Scripts: `scripts/EXP117/` (`chainwheel.js`, `missing.js`, `permute.js`, `words.js`), outputs next to them.

## Measured

- The 141 faces have 268 boundary vertices with one neighbour and 268 with three. The loose ends are
  1.3 mm or more apart, so it isn't a welding gap (`chainwheel.out`).
- All 274 mesh-boundary edges that carry id 0 are in control-0 strips of 3 vertices
  (`missing.out`). Those 276 strips always have words 0 and 1 equal to 0; words 2 and 3 carry ids
  (274 and 225 nonzero, `words.out`).
- Every assignment of the strip's words to the triangle's three edges was tried (`permute.out`).
  Word 2 on (v,v+1), word 3 on (v+1,v+2) and a zero on (v,v+2) closes 189 of 189 faces; the next
  best closes 176; the reader's order closes 48.

## Blind check

The EXP-115 disprove agent found the same swap independently: the 141 failing faces are exactly the
faces with control-0 strips; of the 6 orders only the swap closes all 189; with it every face joins a
native face uniquely by its edge-id set and the loops equal native on 189 of 189
(`scripts/EXP115/disprove/chain3.out`, `chain5.out`). Two other files have control-0 strips
(`通气塞` face 11, `4febfc67` face 1) where words 0 and 1 are both 0, so the swap changes nothing
there.
