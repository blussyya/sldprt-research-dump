# EXP-086 — Three of the five online parts that didn't resolve

**Date**: 2026-10-31. **Code**: solidworks-converters `tool`, d773ed8 and 55bcccb.
**Scripts and output**: `scripts/EXP086/`.

NQ-058 listed five online parts whose B-rep didn't resolve. Three now do, and the change helps two
parts that used to resolve with badly wrong curves.

## d2515121: a swept surface cut by a plane

Edge 21786 is a closed loop, the intersection of a SWEPT_SURF with a plane. The march round it
never got back to its start: the loop is a 0.3 × 0.7 mm oval and the step kept missing the stop
test (`ring.js`, `nq058.js`).

The swept surface's profile is a rational quadratic B-spline (a full ellipse, weights 1 and
√2/2), and it already lies in the cutting plane, z = −1.6 mm. The sweep direction is the plane's
normal (`sw.js`). A swept surface cut by any plane is the profile projected along the sweep onto
that plane. The projection is affine, so a rational B-spline maps to one with the same knots and
weights and projected control points. That's exact, and the converter now does it for any swept
surface and plane before it tries a march. Fit error on the part: 9.7e-12 m.

The volume still doesn't compute on this part: the seam split stops on face 3474 ("chain does not
end on a cut line"). That's NQ-057 territory.

## 3c4752f1: a rolling-ball blend that touches a plane at one point

Edge 1655 is a plane meeting a 2 mm rolling-ball blend (supports a cone and a cylinder). The
spine's distance to the plane reaches 2.000000001 mm at exactly one point, which is the edge's
start vertex 2466 (`sp.js`). So the true blend touches the plane there. The fitted B-spline copy
of the blend missed it by 3.1 µm, and the corrector found no common point.

`footOn` already handles a constant-radius blend exactly (a pipe round its spine). Intersections
now use the raw blend instead of the fitted one whenever the radius is constant. The part
resolves.

## 0739c559: the same cylinder twice, and a tolerant vertex

Edge 2277 is the contact curve of a blend with cylinder node 2494. The blend's supports are
cylinders 2513 and 2512, and 2512 has exactly the same radius, axis and point as 2494 (`bb.js`).
SolidWorks wrote the same surface twice. The support is now matched by geometry when the node
isn't one of the blend's own.

Next, edge 6076 (a trimmed ellipse) has vertex 6150 30.3 µm off the curve. That vertex has its own
tolerance, 48.2 µm, so it's within what the file allows. The check now accepts a vertex off its
curve by up to its own tolerance.
