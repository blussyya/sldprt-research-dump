# EXP-077 — Where the remaining geometry is stored

Captured 2026-10-03 UTC. Base: staging `42a2e8b`.

Run `node knowledge/evidence/scripts/EXP077/locate.js --write` from the repository root.
[Script](scripts/EXP077/locate.js), [module snapshot](scripts/EXP077/lib/README.md),
[raw results with input hashes](scripts/EXP077/RESULTS.json). The package tests the pre-2011 half in
`test/display.test.js`, `test/parasolid.test.js`, `test/step-write.test.js` and `test/blast.test.js`.

## Question

After EXP-076 three gaps remained in reading the corpus's geometry:

1. The three pre-2011 parts had no `Config-0-Partition` and no readable display mesh. Where are
   their body and mesh, and how are they stored?
2. INTERSECTION curves block exact STEP on most real parts. What exactly is stored for them?
3. 27 edges in Dekor and USB hub TOP have no curve at all. Where is their geometry?

## 1. Pre-2011 parts

### Compression: PKWARE DCL implode

`DisplayLists__Zip` and `Config-0-Body` both begin `01 06` and are not zlib. That is the header of
the PKWARE Data Compression Library "implode" format (literals Huffman-coded, 4 KB dictionary), the
format zlib's `contrib/blast` reads. A decoder was written (`lib/container/blast.js`, checked
against zlib's reference stream `00 04 82 24 25 8f 80 7f` → `AIAIAIAIAIAIA`). On all three streams
it consumes every input byte exactly:

| stream | in | out | starts with |
|---|---|---|---|
| SW2000-s01 `DisplayLists__Zip` | 592 | 1,697 | display list header |
| chainwheel `DisplayLists__Zip` | 79,857 | 212,677 | display list header |
| chainwheel `Config-0-Body` (after a u32le length equal to stream size − 4) | 93,387 | 158,309 | `PS 00 00`: neutral binary transmit |

### Bodies

| part | stream | form | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| chainwheel | `Config-0-Body` | neutral binary, `SCH_1300242_13006` (Parasolid 13.0, written in the base schema itself) | 4,279 nodes, 189 faces, every byte consumed, 13,527 graph checks, 0 failures |
| plate4 | `Default` (the configuration name) | u32le length, then **bare binary**: `B`, int32 description length, all numbers little-endian, `SCH_900203_9008` (Parasolid 9.0) | 277 nodes, 14 faces, every byte consumed, 900 graph checks, 0 failures |
| SW2000-s01 | none | | no body |

**Base-schema files.** With a two-number schema name the header has no node-type count and no type
carries schema data: every layout is the base one. LIST then has twelve fields, the nine later edit
scripts copy plus `current_block`, `current_position` and `finished`. The bytes force this layout:
with nine fields the next node starts on `00 0f 00 00`, which is not a node.

**Schema 9008.** Walked by hand from the first node, then confirmed by parsing to the terminator.
Differences from 13006: BODY has no `nom_geom_state`; FIN has no `attributes_groups`; ATTRIB_DEF has
no `field_names` pointer and 13 legal-owner flags; LIST is the twelve-field form; type 102 is a KEY
string (`ws0.x_b`, the body's key). Field names for the extra fields come from the XT reference
and are candidates; the layouts themselves are fixed by the parse consuming all 9,408 bytes.

**SW2000-s01 is an empty part.** Its `Config-0` feature tree has only `moRefPlane_c` (×3),
`moOriginProfileFeature_c`, lights, units and views. No feature class, no sketch entity. The stored
preview bitmap (`Preview`, 731×505, 8-bit) shows only the origin triad. Its display list decodes to
1,697 bytes with no face records.

### Meshes

| part | layout | result |
|---|---|---|
| chainwheel | SW2011 face record, plus two arrays between Block3 and the bounding record (12-byte stride / kind 100 and 8-byte stride / kind 8), empty on all 189 faces | 189/189 faces, 2,924 triangles, 189 valid bounding records |
| plate4 | strips, positions, normals, then the bounding record directly: no Block1/2/3 and so no edge IDs; the record starts u32 0, 1, 1 and keeps the box at offsets 12/36/60/84 | 14/14 faces, 44 triangles, 14 valid bounding records |

chainwheel has 276 strips with control value **0** instead of 1. All 276 are 3-vertex strips, single
triangles, so their geometry is unambiguous; both strip and fan readings give triangles facing the
stored normals and the same closed mesh. What the value means beyond that is not established.

Cross-checks: face counts equal the native body's (189/189, 14/14). plate4's mesh has the body's
exact box and volume 38,400,000.7 mm³ against the body's exact 38,400,000 (float32 rounding).
chainwheel: 3,768 of 4,658 vertices lie on a B-rep surface within 1 µm; 833 of the other 890 are on
boundary edges, the chord points seen in modern files (EXP-074); 57 interior vertices up to 36 µm off
are unexplained.

plate4 now converts to exact STEP: 14 planes, reads back as its body, 38,400,000 mm³ by our
integrator and by OpenCascade, valid single solid. chainwheel is blocked by INTERSECTION curves like
the modern parts.

## 2. INTERSECTION curves

An INTERSECTION node (type 38) holds its two surfaces (`surface[2]`), a CHART (type 40) and two
LIMITs (type 41, `start`, `end`). The CHART holds `chart_count` hvecs (points), `base_parameter`,
`base_scale`, and the errors the points were made to: `chordal_error`, `angular_error`,
`parameter_error[2]`.

| part | curves | chart points | points per chart | max chordal_error | worst distance to either surface |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pocket Wheel | 204 | 1,332 | 2–13 | 1.4 mm | 2.7e-15 m |
| chainwheel | 120 | 496 | 4–6 | 1.7 mm | 2.8e-17 m |
| Helical Bevel Gear | 24 | 96 | 4 | 2.5 mm | 2.9e-17 m |
| distributor | 20 | 120 | 2–20 | 0.29 mm | 4.2e-17 m |
| USB hub TOP | 10 (2 skipped: one surface is a blend) | 34 | 3–6 | 0.98 mm | 2.7e-17 m |
| Dekor | 8 | 48 | 6 | 0.23 mm | 4.4e-16 m |
| USB hub BOTTOM | 8 | 44 | 5–7 | 0.71 mm | 4.3e-16 m |

Every chart point is on both surfaces to double precision, so the chart is a set of exact points on
the true intersection curve. The chords between them are far too coarse to write (up to 2.5 mm), so
exact output means marching the surface-surface intersection between them to a chosen tolerance.

## 3. Tolerant edges

All 27 edges without a curve (20 Dekor, 7 USB hub TOP) have a nonzero `tolerance` (5e-7 / 5.05e-7 m
in Dekor, 1e-5 m in USB TOP) and keep one curve per fin:

```
FIN.curve → TRIMMED_CURVE (point_1, point_2, parm_1, parm_2)
          → SP_CURVE (surface, b_curve) → B_CURVE → NURBS_CURVE, vertex_dim 2
```

The NURBS curve is 2D, in the (u,v) parameters of the fin's own face surface (planes, cylinders,
tori and B-spline surfaces here).

| check | result |
|---|---|
| fin curve evaluated through its surface at parm_1/parm_2 vs stored point_1/point_2 | 54/54, worst 1.2e-16 m |
| the two fin curves of each edge, sampled at 2,001 points, distance from one to the other | 27/27 within the edge's tolerance; worst 0.21 µm (Dekor, tolerance 0.5 µm), 1.76 µm (USB TOP, tolerance 10 µm) |

The trim-point match also confirms that Parasolid's (u,v) conventions for those four surface types
are the ones the package's volume integrator uses.

## What this establishes

- Every geometry-bearing stream in the corpus is now located and read. The pre-2011 forms are PKWARE
  implode, a base-schema neutral binary file, and a Parasolid 9 little-endian bare binary file.
- SW2000-s01 contains no solid.
- INTERSECTION curves are stored as two surfaces plus exact sample points; tolerant edges as one 2D
  parameter-space curve per face, agreeing within the edge tolerance.
- What remains for exact STEP on the real parts is writing (marching intersections, emitting surface
  curves) and evaluating SWEPT_SURF and BLENDED_EDGE, not locating data.

No invariant is promoted: one file per pre-2011 form is not a population.

## Limits

- One file each for the base-schema and the 9008 forms; other Parasolid 9–13 era files may use node
  types this corpus doesn't contain.
- Strip control 0, the two empty arrays in chainwheel's records, and 57 off-surface interior vertices
  are open.
- The CHART's `base_parameter`, `base_scale` and the LIMIT contents are read but not interpreted.
