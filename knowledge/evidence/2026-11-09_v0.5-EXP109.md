# EXP-109: Block3 strip controls and the tangent 0/0 exceptions

The strip-control byte is not a per-strip property. The first control is 1 on exactly the faces
that have any nonzero Block3 byte. The later controls are 4 or 0 for a whole file, and the choice
follows the display-list version (`_DL_VERSION_*`). The 12 tangent edges with bytes 0/0 all have a
B-spline face on one side. Nothing in the strips (winding, restarts, degenerate triangles)
separates byte 4 from byte 0.

This is NQ-031's remainder. It builds on EXP-088 and was not sent to blind prove/disprove agents.
Its one independent check is a second read of the raw bytes (below). Treat it as a draft.

Scripts and outputs are in `scripts/EXP109/` (`out_*.txt` next to each script). Reader: `conv-dev`
(`dev`, `src/display.js`). Corpus: 222 part paths, 21,088 faces with an edge table.

## Strip controls (measured)

Block1 holds, per strip of n vertices, 2n-2 words: one control, then edge labels. Block3 has one
byte per Block1 word, so the byte at the strip start is the strip's control byte.

| strip | byte 0 | byte 1 | byte 4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| first strip of a face | 18,398 | 2,690 | 0 |
| later strip of a face | 128,142 | 0 | 16,455 |

(`survey.js`.) A second route reproduces these four counts exactly: `rawcheck.js` reads Block2
lengths and Block3 bytes straight from the inflated display stream and does not use the parser's
`block3` or `stripLengths`.

### The first control is a per-face flag

- The 2,690 faces with a first-control byte of 1 are exactly the 2,690 faces that have any nonzero
  Block3 byte. No face has an edge byte without the flag (`out_faceflags.txt`).
- 852 flagged faces have no edge byte at all. The flag does not just repeat "this face has a
  smooth or open edge".
- Within a face the later controls are all 4 or all 0. No face mixes them (`survey.js` per-face
  patterns: 692 faces all-4, 550 all-0, 0 mixed).

### The flag follows surface type, inside files that use it

Only 27 of 222 files have any flagged face. In the other 195 files (8,760 non-planar and 8,733
planar faces) no face is flagged (`planar2.js`, the control). In the 27 files, planarity judged
from the mesh itself (all stored normals equal, vertices on one plane; `planar.js`):

| | flagged | unflagged |
|---|---:|---:|
| non-planar | 1,968 | 0 |
| planar | 722 | 905 |

Every non-planar face is flagged. Planes are all flagged in 9 files, all unflagged in 16, and mixed in 2 (Carter2019: 58 of 64; `51f01927`: 6 of 128). Where the display tag is
readable (`modefaces.js`), tags 4002 to 4007 are flagged 100%: 333, 85, 22, 35, 10 and 10 faces. Tags
4008 to 4010 do not occur in any file that has flags, so they are untested. Tag 4001 is 498 flagged
and 168 unflagged.

This probably explains EXP-088's mixed 0/1 edges: an unflagged plane has only zero bytes, while the
curved face is flagged and carries 1 on the tangent edge. Not checked edge by edge for the 102.

Why a file has flags at all is not known. Flagged files have DL 11000 or later, but so do many
unflagged files (DL 15000: 1 flagged, 54 not; DL 17000: 6 and 8; DL 18000: 9 and 5; DL 19000: 5
and 6; `out_versions.txt`).

### The later control follows the display-list version

Across the 23 flagged files that have a multi-strip flagged face (`versions.js`):

| `_DL_VERSION` | files | later control |
|---|---:|---|
| none (2014 Carter), 3800, 4100, 11000, 15000 | 7 | 0 |
| 17000, 18000, 19000 | 16 | 4 |

(Four more flagged files have only single-strip flagged faces and say nothing.) `1e794aad` has
`_MO_VERSION` 17000 but `_DL_VERSION` 4100 and gives 0, so the key is the DL stream, not the model
stream. No DL 16000 file has flags. The boundary lies between 15000 and 17000. The split is
perfect in this sample, but 7 vs 16 files is a small sample and the DL stamp is a proxy for
whatever the writer actually changed.

The values seen are 0, 1, 2 and 4: single bits, never combined (edge bytes 1 and 2 never share a
word). Reading them as bit flags is a guess. No bit 3 or higher is seen anywhere.

## Strip geometry does not separate byte 4 from byte 0 (measured)

`geom.js` walks every strip using the parser's winding rule (even triangles `i-2,i-1,i`, odd
`i-1,i-2,i`) and compares each triangle's normal with the sum of its three stored vertex normals.

| strips | strips | triangles | degenerate | winding agrees | winding opposes | start touches previous strip |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| in unflagged faces | 142,674 | 771,643 | 2 | 771,629 | 12 | 40.2% |
| first, flagged | 2,690 | 29,754 | 0 | 29,754 | 0 | n/a |
| later, byte 0 | 3,866 | 19,228 | 0 | 19,228 | 0 | 51.8% |
| later, byte 4 | 16,455 | 77,742 | 0 | 77,736 | 6 | 46.9% |

- No winding flip: 6 of 77,742 byte-4 triangles oppose the normals (0.008%), against 12 of 771,643
  in unflagged faces. Byte 4 does not reverse winding (agrees with EXP-088).
- No degenerate triangles in flagged faces. The two in unflagged faces are the only ones in the corpus.
- "Start touches previous strip" is any of the first three vertices of the strip sharing exact
  coordinates with any vertex of the previous strip. It does not separate byte 0 from byte 4
  (51.8% vs 46.9%), and the 40.2% for unflagged strips (its denominator includes first strips) is similar. Restarts look the same either way.

Since the byte is constant per file, a per-strip geometric meaning would have shown up as a
difference between strips inside one file. There is none to find, and the byte 0 and byte 4 files
differ in version, not in strip structure.

## Tangent 0/0 exceptions (measured)

EXP-088 found 13 two-sided edges under 0.05 rad with Block3 bytes 0/0 (10 under 0.001). Recomputed
from its `results.json` with native surface and curve types (`tangent.js`, `wholebspline.js`):

- 12 of the 13 have a B-spline face on one side (bspline+cone 4, bspline+cylinder 4, bspline+torus 4), all in
  `3c4752f1`. All 12 are `spcurve` edges. Max normal angles are 0.00024 to 0.00195 rad.
- The 13th is `ca465363` edge 3127, plane+plane at 0.0333 rad (1.9 degrees). That is a real crease,
  not a tangent edge, so it is not an exception. EXP-088's "13" counted it by threshold.
- In `3c4752f1` alone: 162 of 162 tangent edges between analytic faces have bytes 1/1; 12 of 12
  tangent edges with a B-spline face have 0/0. The 6 sharp B-spline edges are 0/0 as well.
- Across all files with readable metadata, 123 edge uses on flagged B-spline faces (tag 4006) carry bytes
  0 (111) and 2 (12). None carries 1. Blend (4007) faces do carry 1 (370 uses).

So byte 1 is set for tangent edges between analytic and blend faces, and not for edges whose face
is B-spline. This is a rule from one file. `spcurve` and "B-spline face" are the same 12 edges
here, so the data cannot say which one the writer keys on.

## Ruled out

- Byte 4 as a winding reversal: 6 of 77,742 triangles oppose the normals.
- Byte 4 as a strip restart or degenerate join marker: sharing with the previous strip is 46.9% for 4
  vs 51.8% for 0, no degenerate triangles.
- Strip-control byte as a per-strip property: it is constant per face and per file.
- The first control as an edge-bytes summary: 852 flagged faces have no edge byte.
- The tangent exceptions as unexplained: 12 of 13 are B-spline-adjacent, the 13th is not tangent.

## Not checked yet

- What decides whether a file has flags (27 of 222). Not version alone.
- Why planes are flagged in some files and not others.
- Whether byte 1 absence is "B-spline face" or "`spcurve` edge". Needs a part with an `spcurve` edge between
  analytic faces, or a B-spline face with a non-`spcurve` edge.
- Tags 4008 to 4010 in a flagged file.
- What 4 means beyond "later strip, DL 17000 or later". No file combines DL 16000 with flags. A
  controlled save of one part in two SolidWorks versions would settle the version claim.
- Whether the bit-flag reading holds. A byte of 3, 5, 6 or 8 would test it; none exists here.
- No blind prove/disprove agents were run for this file.

> **Checked 2026-11-09 (blind prove and disprove, own scripts, EXP-109 scripts not read):**
> - The counts reproduce exactly once the 4 byte-identical file pairs are counted once: 18,398 / 2,690 first strips, 128,142 / 16,455 later strips. The first-strip flag rule holds on all 21,113 faces.
> - "Constant per file" is only true for flagged faces. 13 files mix later-strip 0 and 4, but in all 13 the zeros are unflagged faces, whose Block3 bytes are all zero anyway. Among flagged faces with later strips, no file mixes 0 and 4.
> - "Byte 0 on later strips means DL below 17000" needs the same scope. 18 files at DL 17000 or later have only zero later-strip bytes. 14 of them have no flagged face. The other 4 (`7fa6934e`, `951f2bc1`, `3e41786b`, `c5784ab3`) have flagged faces, but none of those faces has a later strip. So no flagged later strip at DL 17000+ carries 0.
> - Byte 4 is not a winding flip, with one exception: in `2e6e140a`, face 555 strip 6 is reversed against the stored normals, and face 560 strip 25 is mixed. Byte-0 strips have 7 mixed-sign strips too, so the normal-sign test is noisy, not a sign of a meaning.
> - Limit: 17 modern files have no readable DisplayLists stream (for example `f750218b`, DL 17000), so they are not in any count.
