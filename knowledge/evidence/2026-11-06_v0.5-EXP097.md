# EXP-097: checking EXP-088 to EXP-096

Every script from EXP-088 to EXP-096 reproduces its saved output, and every blind check below
upheld the main findings. They also found a wrong count, a wrong claim about surface tags, and
limits that the write-ups didn't state. The corrections are made in place in the earlier files,
each with a dated note pointing here.

Scripts and outputs are under `scripts/EXP097/`. The blind checks ran on Haiku, one agent per
claim. Each was given the claim, the data and the code, but not the original scripts or write-up,
and each wrote its own scripts in its own folder.

## The scripts reproduce

All 38 scripts were rerun from a clean checkout.

- Every script that writes its own JSON wrote byte-identical files (`git status` stayed clean).
- Three only print to stdout:
  - `features-blind`: the saved summary matches the printed rows (126 name checks, 0 mismatches).
  - `ghost-production-independent`: also matches (cube: 0 bodies; gear: 7 bodies, 7 references,
    nothing unmatched).
  - `history-independent`: identical when given its four files.
- Three are helpers that need a part file as an argument.

One fault: four EXP-088 scripts loaded the converter from `/workspace/solidworks-converters`, a
path that only existed where they were written. They now use `SLDPRT_CONVERTER` or the sibling
`solidworks-converters` folder, like the other scripts, and give the same output.

EXP-090 to EXP-096 each say "a fresh blind pass" agreed, but no script or verdict from those
passes was saved, so nobody can check them. The checks below replace them.

**EXP-090, split line** (`blind-split-line/`):
- Holds:
  - The y = 0 side keeps Block1 `1,7,76,0,77,53` (SW2011, face 83) and `1,9,81,0,82,58`
    (SW2022, face 88), with the same four vertices in the same order.
  - The x sides grow from six to eight words.
  - Each Block1 sequence occurs exactly once in the raw stream, at the offset the agent's own
    reader computes.
- Controls: two no-split builds match on every face, and flipping one Block1 word is caught.
- Narrower: Block1 lists the face's own edge IDs and never names a neighbour. The shared top
  edge keeps its ID, which is why Block1 doesn't change. C18 can't show what happens when the
  shared edge itself changes.
- Not checked: the SW2011 face IDs come from the package linker, not raw bytes.

**EXP-091, surface parameters** (`blind-surface-params/`):
- Holds, with the agent's own closed-form evaluators:
  - Cone: 1.087e-9 m over 144 vertices.
  - Sphere: 1.242e-9 m over 5,004.
  - Loft: 2.288e-6 m.
  - Cone and sphere residuals are at float32 rounding.
  - The cone's second array is the signed axial distance, exactly.
  - Corpus counts reproduce: 37 files, 1,545 faces, 205,327 vertices.
- Controls: a radius change of 1e-7 m shows as a 1e-7 m error, and shuffled pairings give
  millimetres.
- Limits:
  - Swapping the arrays gives metres only on the cone; on the sphere and loft the median is
    about 5 mm (max 1.0 and 1.3 cm).
  - Across the corpus, 941 of the 1,545 faces have no native face with the same ID in the body
    the converter reads, and 11 joins pick the wrong face.
  - Boundary vertices can sit well off the surface: 9,250 of 39,529 are over 1e-7 m, up to
    6.9e-4 m on a swept face (Dekor). Interior vertices: 180 of 42,630.
  - One closed periodic B-spline (leva1) stores u up to 2 on a [0, 1] knot range. Wrapping by
    the period brings it to 2.4e-8 m.
  - Untested: planes, cylinders, tori, blends and tags 4008/4010.

**LocalBodies** (`blind-localbodies/`):
- Holds. The agent used its own OLE2 reader, container scanner and grammar.
- Counts:
  - 41 streams in 40 files (`d0d61cde` has two configurations), all ending exactly.
  - 281 entries and 289 sections, all with zero eight-byte tails.
  - Headers: 117 of 10 bytes and 164 of 18. The 8 between-section records are flag 0.
- Controls: 14 synthetic mutations and 8 mutations of each real stream are all rejected.
- The flag:
  - Counts hold: 8 entries are flag 1, 273 are flag 0.
  - A string search shows BODY_IN_LIGHTWEIGHT_PERM absent in 8 flag-0 entries and present in
    the other 265 and in all 8 flag-1 entries.
  - The attribute's value wasn't decoded, so "flag 1 exactly when the value is 0" rests on the
    original reader alone.

**Configuration envelope** (`blind-config-envelope/`):

  - `ac760485` is 4.412 mm past it in −x and 12.684 mm in +z.

**Bounds tail** (`blind-bounds-streams/`):
- Holds: 13,589 records in 118 files, head words `[0,0,1]`, 8 tail combinations, and 2,986 unit
  vectors.
- The axis check:
  - "Perpendicular to the cylinder" was measured against the display record's own axis: 2,406
    of 2,406.
  - Against the native B-rep axis it could only be checked on 740 faces. All 740 pass
    (max 2.82e-8).
  - The display and native axes disagree on 51 of 2,313 matched cylinders.
- What the vector is: on 396 of the 740 it's the direction from the axis to the record's box
  centre. That direction is perpendicular to the axis anyway, so perpendicularity alone doesn't
  name the vector.

**Active geometry** (`blind-active-geometry/`):
- Holds:
  - The agent's own sampler gives the same six sets: 268; 21; {0,1,4}; {0,1,4}; {0,1}; {0,1}.
  - Tied configurations export identical STEP.
  - The converter picks wrong in `118a3cb0` (56.0 mm off) and `289756ce` (0.10 mm off).
- Right by accident: in the other four files its lowest-ID pick lands inside the tied set.
- Controls: two files with ModelHeader (`03afebf4`, `c613782b`) select the same configuration
  as their header. In `c613782b`, configuration 2 matches all 13 display faces' boxes but has
  777 faces, so the face count is what rules it out.
- A dependence: the exact match for 268 relies on four single-vertex loops on cone faces. Without
  them the union is about 2 mm off.
- A gap: the dump's `package/` copy has no selection at all and fails on `289756ce`.

**Duplicate streams** (`blind-bounds-streams/`):
- Holds: the agent's own byte scan gives 7,374 valid nonempty entries, 521 empty, and no
  duplicate names, with the same name sets as the reader in every file.
- The count of modern containers is 178, not 183. EXP-095 counted every non-OLE2 path,
  including 5 files in `test files online/not-a-part/` that aren't SolidWorks containers (four
  STEP text files and one starting `nFJACwe0`). They hold no entries, so the other numbers stand.
- Wording: the streams use raw DEFLATE. Zlib-wrapped inflation finds nothing.

## Surface tags 4008 and 4010

NQ-030 said tags 4003 to 4009 are identified and evaluated for every type in the corpus. A count
of every display face (`tags/tags.js`) shows otherwise:

| tag | faces | files | native type by face-ID join |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 4007 | 72 | 7 | blend |
| 4008 | 1 | 1 (`2f121983`) | offset |
| 4009 | 408 | 6 | swept |
| 4010 | 290 | 2 (`a4ecc6f5`, `8cd79ff5`) | spun (288; 2 have no native face) |
| 16368, 4980815, 65536 | 22, 33, 1 | 1 each | not joined; probably misread metadata |

- The join (`tags/join.js`, run with the current converter) has a control: the C13 to C16
  controls come out as cone, sphere, torus and B-spline.
- 4008 and 4010 weren't named anywhere before. The format write-up lists 4001 to 4007 and 4009.
- Three faces in the join disagree with their tag. Each has an ID that two display faces share,
  so the join is ambiguous there. That doesn't contradict the tag mapping.
- Display face IDs aren't unique in `2f121983` and `a4ecc6f5`. That's worth its own look.
