# EXP-121: head_face's SP_CURVE edges millimetres to centimetres off

2026-11-26. Three causes, all in how the converter places SP_CURVEs on blends it fits itself, and
not in the file:

1. **A blend surface shared by two faces was fitted for the first face only.** The second face's
   edges were evaluated past the end of that fit, up to 4.2 cm off. Fixed: each face gets its own fit.
2. **Re-anchoring picked the wrong contact side.** Parasolid's v runs from either support depending on
   the blend, and the code took the side from it. Six edges were 1.4 to 2.4 mm off. Fixed: a contact
   edge takes the side its vertices are on.
3. **Diagonal SP_CURVEs.** Some edges cross the blend from one section to another. Re-anchoring
   assumes a single section and can't fit them. Where the edge has a fin on a surface we evaluate
   exactly, that fin fits within 1e-8 m (fix in test). Three blend-to-blend edges have no such fin.

Along the way: Parasolid's blend v is the angle fraction across the section, from one support,
the same support for every edge of a blend. Its u is close to the spine's arc length, 0.08 to 0.23%
off on long edges, so not exact.

Scripts: `scripts/EXP121/`, outputs next to them. Converter commits 32426cb (causes 1 and 2) and the
fin fallback (cause 3, sweep running).

## Parasolid's (u, v) on a rolling-ball blend

`uv_corpus.js` takes every SP_CURVE edge on a constant-radius blend with two vertices. It compares
the 2D (u, v) at each end with the vertex's place on our fitted blend. The online sample outside the
Poppy files has 2 such edges (both sections in `2f121983`, du = 0, v equal to the angle fraction to
3e-8). head_face and head_back have 57 (`uv_poppy.jsonl`, `uv_poppy.summary`):

- **v is the angle across the section as a fraction, from one of the two supports.** 15 edges match
  the fraction from support 0 and 18 from support 1, within 1e-3. The other 24 match one or the other
  within 7e-3, about what vertex tolerances of 1e-5 to 4e-5 m allow on a 2 mm blend. No blend mixes
  the two sides. Which support it starts from isn't decoded.
- **u is close to the spine's arc length in metres, not equal.** Of the 15 edges whose ends sit
  more than 1e-5 m apart along the spine, 11 give |du| / arc length of 1.0008 to 1.0023, edge 1232
  gives 1.0127, and the three shortest (1.0e-5 and 1.0e-4 m) give 1.616, 0.998 and 0.9936. That
  isn't good enough to place a point to 1e-8 m.

## 3. Diagonal edges

Six over-bar edges have 2D u spans of 1e-4 to 1.8e-3. Their vertices lie on sections of the spine
that are 1.5 to 1.7 mm apart (1192, 1232, 1206), 0.1 mm apart (1171, 11067) and 3.8 µm apart
(11272). The re-anchored errors equal those distances (`sect.out`). The edges cross the blend
diagonally. A single section can't fit them, and the u reading above isn't exact enough to map them.

Each of these edges also has a fin, the edge as its other face stores it. Fitted from that fin
where it lies on a surface we evaluate exactly (`fins.out`):

| edge | other fin's surface | on the blend | from the fin |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 1192 | B-spline 4041 | 2.38e-2 m | 3.87e-9 m |
| 1232 | B-spline 4042 | 1.10e-2 m | 3.95e-9 m |
| 1206 | B-spline 4040 | 6.43e-3 m | 7.43e-9 m |
| 11862 | offset 15920 | 2.73e-3 m | 1.37e-11 m (ends within 2.7e-7 m of vertices of tolerance 4e-5) |
| 1171, 11067, 11272 | blends | 9.8e-5, 8.6e-5, 3.8e-6 m | no exact fin |

The converter now does this when an SP_CURVE on a fitted blend misses the bar. It keeps whichever
fit is better and reports that edge's distance from its other faces, as for tolerant edges. This is
EXP-113's finding put to use: off the blend, the edge is its neighbour's fin.
