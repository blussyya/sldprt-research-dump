## Results

All 49 controlled SLDPRT files yield a complete typed **first** Parasolid payload in `Config-0-Partition`: 8,209 nodes, 287 FACE records, and 14,262 passing topology checks. Every payload has a WORLD root which references its one BODY. Face counts equal the standalone export counts already checked against the build log by EXP-071. Both OLE2 and modern containers are exercised.

For the controlled wrapper, the first zlib member begins at stream offset 28. Its consumed compressed length is recorded using the inflater, and every remaining stream byte is preserved as `trailerHex`. **That remainder can contain further compressed members; it is not asserted to be padding or decoded by this experiment.** The map's offsets are relative to the inflated first payload, not physical SLDPRT offsets.

The comparison starts from each BODY and pairs pointer targets by field role. It maintains a bijection and compares types, scalar values, array values and selected graph links. It does not use equal serialization indices as evidence of entity identity. Attributes, session ownership, geometry-owner rings, and derived geometry-data caches are explicitly excluded; the BODY field selection is visible in the script.

47/49 selected body graphs match their standalone X_B exports. Both lofts fail this stricter comparison: native `nom_geom_state` is 2 versus exported 1, and several curve references, types, and aliases differ. All topology nodes are visited, but geometric graph equality is **not** established for those two files. The differences are preserved rather than suppressed.

The native C00 also passes EXP-071's plane/loop/orientation/volume checks. This demonstrates decoded native solid topology and point/plane geometry on that test vector. It does not establish which payload is authoritative across configurations, rollback states, GhostPartition, or delta-labelled streams, nor that every native part has its saved solid in this first payload.
